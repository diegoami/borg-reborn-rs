## The blocking review finding and its fix

PR #135's first review showed that T3.7 could alternate Ascend/Descend
indefinitely without advancing game time. It reset the no-time budget,
refreshed Descend's depth measure on every oscillation, fired on fresh
unmapped levels, and was invisible to the Hold-based report. The rejected
references contained 2,040 same-turn stair actions, including 249 level
changes at one turn in one run.

The new baseline (`9644204`) has **83 stair actions** and no turn above
the declared cap of two. Its guards are independent:

- every level has a unique generation ID, and no travel goal survives a
  generation change: a fresh level with a frontier Explores;
- only an exhausted frontier or a failed/cooling Explore goal may reroll,
  at most once per level generation;
- `Memory` counts level changes separately, so stairs cannot reset the
  ordinary no-time budget; two changes at one turn trigger a visible,
  time-consuming backstop and cool Descend;
- unsafe staircases retreat rather than becoming permanent Holds;
- remembered-monster danger uses the track's existing confidence decay,
  rather than blocking an exit forever after the monster has left view.

The 22 reroll-cap activations in the final full suite are deliberate,
traced backstops, not uncounted loops. Four other action refusals remain
for M4's tactical layer.
