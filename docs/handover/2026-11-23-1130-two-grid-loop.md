**Codex review of #1152 (`440d7938`), three P2 findings, fixed.**
1. **One step charged per continuation.** A rebuild's own first step (its
   stair search calls back into `borg_flow_old`) is only the step; the outer
   call does the accounting once (`advance`).
2. **The commitment is tied to its stair walk.** It is recorded where the
   brave think's stair search succeeds (`commit_brave_walk` at the up, any
   and down branches, only while the avoidance is boosted), no longer after
   any brave action such as an attack. Each committed flow bumps
   `FlowState::commit_seq`, so another flow taking over (a breeder exit, a
   kill flow) ends the walk (`own_flow`).
3. **A down-stair walk is rebuilt toward down stairs.** The walk keeps the
   stair search that started it (`BraveStairs`: up, any, down), and
   `brave_walk_respread` uses it again.
