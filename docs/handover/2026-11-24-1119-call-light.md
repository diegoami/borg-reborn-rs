## #1119 Discretionary lighting in glowing rooms

The radius-one lighting check requires an unglowing casting square or
observed unglowing floor within the spell's light sphere on a first cast.
Call Light reaches one grid and Light Room reaches two. Both spells can
also flood a room through `LIGHT_AREA`. The confirmed-cast cache still
allows a recast when new unglowing floor becomes observable. Larger
carried-light radii, combat casting, and other light sources keep their
own checks. See the [latest task review](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-reborn-rs/pull/1166#issuecomment-7136541562).

The confirmed-cast rule is inherited from #1115. At
`216e0cef181d5183079b1fb80a1894935f476ed8`, the review's diagnostic
observations found that recasts left the mapped unglowing-floor set
unchanged on 26 of 105 total Call Light attempts for seed 2613747 and
71 of 287 for seed 694417276. [#1210](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-reborn-rs/issues/1210)
tracks whether to narrow that rule while preserving room flooding.

`borg_param_light_first_cast_require_dark=1` ships in `harness/borg.txt`.
Set it to 0 to restore the first-cast behavior before #1119 while keeping
the confirmed-cast cache. [Zoo #755](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-zoo/issues/755)
can compare 0 and 1 on one pin with `cave_light_engine_radius=1`. Its
diagnostic arm uses both settings at 0.

Cemetery's Half-Troll Priest 694417276 and Zoo's Half-Troll Priest 2613747
were used as named development replays. See [Cemetery's original
study](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-cemetery/pull/138) and
[Zoo's Priest comparison](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-zoo/issues/736#issuecomment-7111965488).
