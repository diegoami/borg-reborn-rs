## #1119 Discretionary lighting in glowing rooms

The radius-one lighting check now requires a dark casting square or an
observed dark floor on the first cast from a square. The existing
confirmed-cast cache still allows a recast when new dark floor becomes
observable. Radius-two lighting and combat casting keep their own checks.

Cemetery's Half-Troll Priest 694417276 and Zoo's Half-Troll Priest 2613747
were used as named development replays. See [Cemetery's original
study](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-cemetery/pull/138) and
[Zoo's Priest comparison](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-zoo/issues/736#issuecomment-7111965488).

On 2613747, Call Light attempts fell from 147 to 90 and mana-rest notes
from 61 to 33. Both runs survived at CL4 and deepest DL2 at 60,000 turns.
On 694417276, attempts were 264 and 278, and mana-rest notes 105 and 76.
Both survived to 100,000 turns and reached DL2. The candidate reached
CL6 against CL5 with the previous code. These are named regressions.
A paired Zoo Priest comparison is needed to assess the class-wide effect.
