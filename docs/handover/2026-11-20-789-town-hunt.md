## #789 ranged reserve and town hunt

Four parameters compile and ship at zero. The live shopping planner can
reserve throw-ready oil and launcher-compatible ammunition, with
`town_ranged_reserve__<class>` for an individual class. The town hunt uses
the four `town-melee-only` entries in the shipped scary list. It checks the
highest contact danger first, prefers ranged attacks by a configurable
amount, and rechecks the #759 forecast each turn. Farmer Maggot is avoided.
A losing close fight or HP below the configured floor returns to the town
flight flow. The legacy list remains effective when the hunt is disabled.

The seven #604 cases ran to 100,000 engine turns on main `d7e58cdf` and
implementation `8e0f20af`. The trial used a reserve of 6, a 50% ranged
score bonus and a 50% HP floor. Town ↔ DL1 transitions went from 774 to
575, finish-shopping events from 183 to 12, and deaths were zero in both
arms. Two starts had more town ↔ DL1 transitions. All three smoke games
matched main with the feature off. Per-seed counts and examples are in
[PR #800](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-reborn-rs/pull/800).

Population measurement is requested in
[borg-zoo #538](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-zoo/issues/538), pinned
to `8e0f20af735b5c19986fdc119deb04cc0ed64391`. The owner schedules the
pilot and decides whether to change the shipped settings.
