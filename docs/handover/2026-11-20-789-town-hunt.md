## #789 ranged reserve and town hunt

Four parameters compile and ship at zero. The live shopping planner can
reserve throw-ready oil and launcher-compatible ammunition, with
`town_ranged_reserve__<class>` for an individual class. The town hunt uses
the four `town-melee-only` entries in the shipped scary list. It checks the
highest contact danger first, prefers ranged attacks by a configurable
amount, and rechecks the #759 forecast each turn. Farmer Maggot is avoided.
A losing close fight or HP below the configured floor returns to the town
flight flow. The legacy list remains effective when the hunt is disabled.

The named-case replay arm uses a reserve of 6, a 50% ranged score bonus
and a 50% HP floor. These are trial values. The pull request records smoke,
replay results and the borg-zoo measurement request. The latter covers the
population check under RULES 20.
