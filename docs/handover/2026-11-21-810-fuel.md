fuel-burning lantern. The planner keeps fuel in the pack when disposing

keeps such stacks, and planned oil throws account for the reserve. A
desperate pack clear or a unique fight can still spend it. A separate
setting adds preference for an offered fueled lantern that is at least
as good as the equipped torch on the normal equipment score.
