## 2026-11-21: Town fuel and spare torches (#810)

The shipped profile protects 20,000 fuel turns. A character carrying a
fresh equipped torch has room in that reserve for three fresh spares.
The Borg replenishes after immediate needs and values additional fuel
on a diminishing curve. The lantern shopping bonus stays at zero.

The named Ranger control ran out after putting its spare torches in
Home. The [PR](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-reborn-rs/pull/835)
records the named checks and the engine's per-torch fuel behavior.

The paired [Zoo pilot](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-zoo/issues/577)
covers cross-class outcomes and the retained departure fuel stream.
The owner can use those outcomes to revisit the target.
