## 2026-11-21: departure room, a Word of Recall curve and surplus-first storing (#859)

Owner: a Borg with 20,000 gold did not buy a Word of Recall, so the dive gate
(`prepared_recall_depth`, DL5) held it shallow; "purchases are stopped
because of carry weight". With #711's `town_store_only_overweight` (shipped 1)
storing stopped as soon as the pack was under its allowance (`BI_CARRY / 2`),
so a pack at the allowance had no room for a 0.5 lb scroll.

Done, on `task/T859-town-room` from track-p `562979d3`, merged with track-p `29c44cbc` (#860's sync with main) after review:

- `town_departure_free_weight` (tenths of a pound) and
  `town_departure_free_slots`: `dispositions` stores at the Home until this
  room, plus what the Recall reserve still needs (`recall_room`), is free;
  `step`'s purchases and the Home withdrawals leave the room free. Short of
  slots, whole stacks go. A departure notes the room left once a visit
  ("# Town departure room: ...").
- `town_store_keep_min`: short of weight, the copies of a stack past this
  many go first, priced by one copy on their curve; only those copies count
  as freed. Review F1 (#864) read the plan's action as storing the whole
  stack, and it did: `first_action` built it from the kit row. The surplus
  now travels with the disposition (`StepOutput::copies`), so the plan's action
  stores exactly the surplus (12 copies, 3 kept: 9; the whole stack with
  `town_store_keep_min` 0 or an unknown flavour). The **live** path never
  stored a whole stack: `evaluate` moves one copy of every action but a
  purchase and solves again (#507; now `executed_copies`, tested), so the
  stack goes down copy by copy through its surplus. The re-review of
  `2525f38f` found it did not stop at the minimum: at exactly
  `town_store_keep_min` copies the stack was no longer a surplus, was priced
  whole, counted a slot as freed and went home one copy per solve, down to
  none. **Now the minimum is a floor** (the owner handed this session the PR
  to finish, 2026-11-22): a stack that serves a need (`need` or `serves`:
  potions, scrolls, torches, food) or a stack of flasks of oil (by tval;
  owner on the re-review of `c2dc7793`, which found three flasks sold:
  "check that flasks are taken"; `flasks_are_floored_too`), with a known
  flavour, is kept whole at or below the minimum while storing for weight
  (not stored, sold or dropped), so it frees no slot and the
  live walk stops at three (`the_live_walk_stops_at_the_minimum` replays it
  one solve per copy). An item that serves no need (a lone armour piece)
  has no floor (`an_item_that_serves_no_need_has_no_floor`), and short of
  slots whole stacks still go. A pack whose only storable weight is floored
  stacks leaves with less room than the target. Not changed: the plan
  counts the whole surplus as freed while the live executor moves one copy,
  so the purchase limits of that step have headroom that is not there yet;
  every solve after a copy moves sees the real pack.
  The floor changes live play, so the zoo arms of borg-zoo#612 (run on
  `c7ea18df`) predate it. The plan record is not
  changed (`PLAN_SCHEMA` stays 12): the dispose codes are the same and the
  quantity follows from the kit's count and the setting.
- `town_recall_curve_scale_percent` (owner: 110, "use the trip weight"):
  above 0 every scroll is valued on a diminishing curve on the trip weight,
  never zero, with the reserve a requirement on top (`RECALL_RESERVE`, as
  Phase Door's; `recall_curve`). 0 is #557. A first version scaled the
  reserve's requirement instead, which cut the value to zero past
  scale / 100 scrolls; the owner wanted decreasing value, not a cut-off.
- `town_recall_urgent` (owner: "Word of recall should have a high value"):
  when the next trip reaches the dive gate and none is carried, the first
  scroll is urgent (bought first; the gold floor does not hold that scroll
  back, but still caps the spares and the reserve requirement).
  With the curve on, Recall's trip weight grows with the trip's exposure.
- The valuation of the other objects (zero unless their condition is
  present now) is left to #863, which the owner says needs a redesign.
- Compiled defaults (0, 0, 0, 0, 0) play as before. `harness/borg.txt` ships
  50, 2, 3, 110 and urgent 1; `smoke-borg-rs` is re-recorded in the same PR.
- The storing settings reach `dispositions_by` as a `Storing` value, so the
  unit tests can set them (the registry gives tests compiled defaults).
