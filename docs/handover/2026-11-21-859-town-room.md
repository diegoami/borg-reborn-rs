## 2026-11-21: departure room, a Word of Recall curve and surplus-first storing (#859)

Owner: a Borg with 20,000 gold did not buy a Word of Recall, so the dive gate
(`prepared_recall_depth`, DL5) held it shallow; "purchases are stopped
because of carry weight". With #711's `town_store_only_overweight` (shipped 1)
storing stopped as soon as the pack was under its allowance (`BI_CARRY / 2`),
so a pack at the allowance had no room for a 0.5 lb scroll.

Done, on `task/T859-town-room` from track-p `562979d3`:

- `town_departure_free_weight` (tenths of a pound) and
  `town_departure_free_slots`: `dispositions` stores at the Home until this
  room, plus what the Recall reserve still needs (`recall_room`), is free;
  `step`'s purchases and the Home withdrawals leave the room free. Short of
  slots, whole stacks go. A departure notes the room left once a visit
  ("# Town departure room: ...").
- `town_store_keep_min`: short of weight, the copies of a stack past this
  many go first, priced by one copy on their curve; only those copies count
  as freed (the executor stores one copy and solves again).
- `town_recall_curve_scale_percent` (owner: 110, "use the trip weight"):
  above 0 every scroll is valued on a diminishing curve on the trip weight,
  never zero, with the reserve a requirement on top (`RECALL_RESERVE`, as
  Phase Door's; `recall_curve`). 0 is #557. A first version scaled the
  reserve's requirement instead, which cut the value to zero past
  scale / 100 scrolls; the owner wanted decreasing value, not a cut-off.
- Compiled defaults (0, 0, 0, 0) play as before. `harness/borg.txt` ships
  50, 2, 3 and 110; `smoke-borg-rs` is re-recorded in the same PR.
- The storing settings reach `dispositions_by` as a `Storing` value, so the
  unit tests can set them (the registry gives tests compiled defaults).
