## 2026-11-23: The fast-fragile heal floor in contact, by HP-to-damage ratio (#1065)

Every check below ran on the **default core, borg-rs** (`borg-core/`).
No check ran on the reborn core: this change does not touch it.

The branch is `track-p` `bf8d2604` plus two commits, cherry-picked from
PR #1076: the rule, and the fix after its Codex review. #1076 had grown
two merges of `track-p`. This base already ships #1074's #926 bundle
(`fast_fragile_heal_floor = 50`, `fast_fragile_escape_quantile = 75`,
`fast_fragile_count_dot = 1`), so the shipped play is the bundle.

### What was done

One new parameter, `fast_fragile_heal_min_ratio` (tenths). The compiled
default and the shipped value in `harness/borg.txt` are both **0**: the
play of `track-p` before this change.

With N > 0 and the heal floor on, the floor lifts the heal ban only if the
cure buys turns. This applies **in contact**, meaning an awake fast-fragile
monster (crow, wild cat) is adjacent. A healing action then runs only if

    min(max HP, HP + heal) * 10 >= N * damage

- **heal** is the heal of the action being taken (review of #1076, P2).
  `heal_ban` returns a `HealBan`: `Free`, `Banned`, or `Ratio` in contact
  at the floor. `borg_heal` and `borg_heal_outmatched` ask `ban.allows(heal)`
  before each cure, with that cure's own heal:
  - Cure Light Wounds 20, Cure Serious Wounds 40, Cure Critical Wounds 60.
    These are exact: `HEAL_HP` dice 20/40/60 with no percentage
    (`lib/gamedata/object.txt:3165-3166`, `:3184-3185`, `:3201-3202`), and
    the cure activations heal the same (`activation.txt`).
  - Minor Healing heals max(CL + 10, missing HP × (CL/4)%)
    (`class.txt:742-746`).
  - Every heal is capped at max HP (`effect_handler_HEAL_HP`,
    `effect-handler-attack.c:201-232`).

  So a CLW that does not buy turns no longer blocks a CSW the caution's own
  gates choose: at 15/60 against 12 a turn, a CLW leaves 2.9 turns and a CSW
  4.6. Attack on sight steps aside (`yield_to_cure`) when the largest cure
  carried passes.
- **damage** is the next turn's damage of the fast-fragile monsters within
  two grids: the mean, or the `fast_fragile_escape_quantile` quantile. It is
  the monsters' part of "the next turn kills" (`monsters_damage`, split out
  of `next_turn_damage`). The Borg's own cut and poison are left out,
  because the cure stops or shortens them.

When not even a full heal buys the turns, the ban holds as before, and the
escape (Phase Door, the stairs underfoot) or the attack decides. The trace
names both cases:
- "`# Fast-fragile in contact, HP a/b: at the heal floor, a cure is allowed
  if it leaves ... (#1065)`";
- "`# Fast-fragile in contact, HP a/b: even a full heal leaves ... no cure at
  the heal floor (#1065)`".

These cases keep their current behaviour:
- **Out of contact** (the crow at range in 592754909), the floor yields at
  any ratio, as the issue asks.
- **Bleeding out** (`fast_fragile_count_dot`, `dot_kills`) still lifts the
  ban at any ratio: the cure that stops the bleeding is not a race.
- The `fast_fragile_no_heal = 2` cornered exception is not touched.

With N = 0 the ban is `Free` or `Banned` exactly as `heal_banned` was, and
`yield_to_cure` does not read it, so play stays byte for byte the same.

The unit tests (`tests_1065`, at N = 30):
- **The issue's fatal points.** 10 to 17 max HP against 7.2 to 8.0 a turn:
  the ban holds. The Mage 645909552 (26 against 6.4): the cure is allowed,
  even from 2 HP. The Blackguard 2025089708 (60 against 10 to 14): a CLW at
  15 HP is allowed against 10 and not against 12; a CSW is allowed.
- **The review's both-potions case.** `each_cure_asks_the_ban_with_its_own_heal`.
- **Minor Healing's amount.**

### The issue's third bullet (the rest), not done here: #1081

"A rest is never chosen below the floor while a fast-fragile monster was
seen within `fast_fragile_memory` turns (#982)." No such parameter exists.
#999 (`recover_rest_cure_hp_percent`, shipped 50) drinks instead of resting
below its floor when a fast awake monster is known within 20 grids. That
covers 2125189042.

The review of #1076 (P3) noted that #999 is not the same rule: with no cure
it still rests, and it keys on a monster known now, not on a recent
sighting. That gap is **#1081**, for the owner to decide.

### What is left, and for the owner

- **Measurement:** borg-zoo#717, re-pinned to this branch. The control is
  the shipped bundle, against `fast_fragile_heal_min_ratio = 30`; a pilot,
  then full. It asks for the count of the two trace notes above, to see how
  often the gate is reached.
- **Testing through `borg_heal`.** The review asked for the both-potions
  case to be covered through the healing decision. borg-core's engine-free
  release unit tests cannot call `borg_heal`, which draws the engine's RNG
  (`Rand_div`). So the test covers the ban with the review's numbers, and
  each call site is a `ban.allows(heal) &&` in front of a cure action. No
  game here reaches the gate, so it is not exercised in play either.
- **Overlap.** #946 (`fast_fragile_contact_max`) also changes the heal ban
  (`heal_banned`, now `heal_ban`). Whichever merges second resolves the
  conflict. Should the ratio also apply to its group-contact lift? That is
  the owner's call.
- **30 is a hypothesis**, the issue's proposal: no measurement chose it.
