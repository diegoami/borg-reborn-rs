## 2026-11-23: The fast-fragile heal floor in contact, by HP-to-damage ratio (#1065)

Every check below ran on the **default core, borg-rs** (`borg-core/`),
built from `origin/track-p` `8f32a8a0` plus this branch. No check ran on
the reborn core: this change does not touch it.

### What was done

One new parameter, `fast_fragile_heal_min_ratio` (tenths). The compiled
default and the shipped value in `harness/borg.txt` are both **0**, today's
play.

With N > 0 and the heal floor on (`fast_fragile_heal_floor` above 0), the
floor lifts the heal ban only if the cure buys turns. This applies **in
contact**, meaning an awake fast-fragile monster is adjacent. In both
places it acts, `heal_banned` (the caution's cures) and `yield_to_cure`
(attack on sight stepping aside), the floor then needs

    min(max HP, HP + heal) * 10 >= N * damage

- **heal** is the heal of the action about to be taken (review of #1076,
  P2). `heal_ban` returns a `HealBan`: `Free`, `Banned`, or `Ratio` in
  contact at the floor. `borg_heal` and `borg_heal_outmatched` ask
  `ban.allows(heal)` before each cure with that cure's own heal:
  - Cure Light Wounds 20, Cure Serious Wounds 40, Cure Critical Wounds 60.
    These are exact: `HEAL_HP` dice 20/40/60 with no percentage
    (`lib/gamedata/object.txt:3165-3166`, `:3184-3185`, `:3201-3202`), and
    the cure activations heal the same (`activation.txt`).
  - Minor Healing heals max(CL + 10, missing HP × (CL/4)%)
    (`class.txt:742-746`).
  - Every heal is capped at max HP (`effect_handler_HEAL_HP`,
    `effect-handler-attack.c:201-232`).

  So a CLW that does not buy turns no longer blocks a CSW the caution's own
  gates choose: at 15/60 against 12 a turn, a CLW leaves 2.9 turns and a
  CSW 4.6. Attack on sight steps aside when the largest cure carried
  passes. When not even a full heal buys the turns, the ban holds.
- **damage** is the next turn's damage of the fast-fragile monsters within
  two grids: the mean, or the `fast_fragile_escape_quantile` quantile. It
  is the monsters' part of "the next turn kills" (`monsters_damage`,
  split out of `next_turn_damage`). The Borg's own cut and poison are left
  out, because the cure stops or shortens them.

Below the ratio the ban holds, and the escape (Phase Door, the stairs
underfoot) or the attack decides. The trace says so: "a cure leaves X HP
against Y damage/turn, Z turns, under N/10; no cure at the heal floor
(#1065)".

These cases keep their current behaviour:
- **Out of contact** (the crow at range in 592754909), the floor yields at
  any ratio, as the issue asks.
- **Bleeding out** (`fast_fragile_count_dot`, `dot_kills`) still lifts the
  ban at any ratio: the cure that stops the bleeding is not a race.
- The `fast_fragile_no_heal = 2` cornered exception is not touched.

With N = 0, `yield_to_cure` does not run its contact search, so play stays
byte for byte the same even with the floor on.

The issue's fatal points as unit tests (`tests_1065`, at N = 30):
- 10 to 17 max HP against 7.2 to 8.0 a turn: the ban holds.
- The Mage 645909552 (26 against 6.4): the cure is allowed, even from
  2 HP.
- The Blackguard 2025089708 (60 against 10 to 14): allowed at 35 HP after
  a CLW against 10. Not allowed against 12 (2.9 turns). Allowed after a
  CSW.

### The issue's third bullet (the rest), not done here: #1081

"A rest is never chosen below the floor while a fast-fragile monster was
seen within `fast_fragile_memory` turns (#982)." No such parameter exists.
#999 (`recover_rest_cure_hp_percent`, now merged and shipped 50) drinks
instead of resting below its floor when a fast awake monster is known
within 20 grids, which covers 2125189042.

The review of #1076 (P3) noted that #999 is not the same rule: with no
cure it still rests, and it keys on a monster known now, not on a recent
sighting. That gap is **#1081**, for the owner to decide.

### What is left, and for the owner

- **Measurement.** This should be measured with #926's switches as one
  bundle (the issue), on top of #1074 if it ships the #926 bundle. Arms:
  the bundle alone vs the bundle with `fast_fragile_heal_min_ratio = 30`.
  No zoo request is filed yet; borg-zoo#708 is the study's definition PR,
  and adding the arm there or in a new request is for the owner to say.
- **Testing through `borg_heal`.** The review asked for the both-potions
  case to be covered through the healing decision. borg-core's
  engine-free release unit tests cannot call `borg_heal`, which draws the
  engine's RNG (`Rand_div`). So `each_cure_asks_the_ban_with_its_own_heal`
  covers the ban with the review's numbers, and the call sites are a
  `ban.allows(heal) &&` in front of each cure action. No game here reaches
  the gate, so it is not exercised in play either.
- **Overlap.** #946 (`fast_fragile_contact_max`) also changes
  `heal_banned`. Whichever merges second resolves the conflict; the ratio
  should then apply to its group-contact lift too, which is the owner's
  call.
- **30 is a hypothesis**, the issue's proposal: no measurement chose it.
