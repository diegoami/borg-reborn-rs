## 2026-11-23: The fast-fragile heal floor in contact, by HP-to-damage ratio (#1065)

Every check below ran on the **default core, borg-rs** (`borg-core/`).
No check ran on the reborn core: this change does not touch it.

The branch is `track-p` `bf8d2604` plus two commits, cherry-picked from
PR #1076: the rule, and the fix after its Codex review. #1076 had grown
two merges of `track-p`. After the Codex review of #1089 it merged
`track-p` `09580159`, as the owner asked (#1074's bundle, #1079's light
radius, #1075, #898), and took two more commits: the review fixes, and the
owner's decision to **ship the ratio at 30**. After the Codex re-review it

### What was done

One new parameter, `fast_fragile_heal_min_ratio` (tenths). It is compiled
0, the play of `track-p` before this change. It is **shipped 30** in
`harness/borg.txt` (owner, 2026-11-23, on PR #1089), before any zoo result.

With N > 0 and the heal floor on, the floor lifts the heal ban only if the
cure buys turns. This applies **in contact**, meaning an awake fast-fragile
monster (crow, wild cat) is adjacent. A healing action then runs only if

    min(max HP, HP + heal) * 10 >= N * damage

- **heal** is the heal of the action being taken (reviews of #1076 and
  #1089, P2). `heal_ban` returns a `HealBan`: `Free`, `Banned`, or `Ratio`
  in contact at the floor. Every action `borg_heal` and
  `borg_heal_outmatched` take after the ban is wrapped in
  `ok(action) && ...`, which asks `ban.allows` with that action's engine
  heal (`heal_of(HealAction)`). The heals are the larger of `HEAL_HP`'s
  base and its `m_bonus` percent of the missing HP, capped at max HP
  (`effect_handler_HEAL_HP`, `effect-handler-attack.c:201-232`):
  - potions of Cure Light, Serious and Critical Wounds 20, 40 and 60
    (`object.txt:3165`, `:3184`, `:3201`), and the matching activations;
  - Healing, the Staff of Healing and CURE_FULL 300 or 35% (`:3221`,
    `:4374`, `activation.txt:114`); the Rod of Healing, HEAL1 and HEAL3 500;
  - *Healing* and CURE_FULL2 1200; Life and CURE_NONORLYBIG 5000; HEAL2 and
    Holy Word 1000; the Healing spell 2000;
  - Minor Healing max(CL + 10, missing HP × (CL/4)%) (`class.txt:742-746`);
  - Fast Recovery and CURE_BODY 30;
  - 0 for a cure that heals nothing: Neutralize Poison, Purging, the Staff
    of Curing, CURE_TEMP, Cure Poison, Herbal Curing, Restore Mana.

  So a CLW that does not buy turns no longer blocks a CSW the caution's own
  gates choose (15/60 against 12 a turn: 2.9 turns against 4.6). A Staff of
  Healing that leaves 550 of the 570 HP needed is refused even though a
  full heal would pass. With the ban `Free`, `ok` is always true and the
  actions are tried as before. The 96 wrapped calls were placed by a script
  that classifies each call by its item or spell, and fails on any it does
  not know.
- **damage** is the next turn's damage of the fast-fragile monsters within
  two grids: the mean, or the `fast_fragile_escape_quantile` quantile. It is
  the monsters' part of "the next turn kills" (`monsters_damage`, split out
  of `next_turn_damage`). The Borg's own cut and poison are left out,
  because the cure stops or shortens them.

When not even a full heal buys the turns, the ban holds as before, and the
escape (Phase Door, the stairs underfoot) or the attack decides. The trace
names both cases:
- "`# Fast-fragile in contact, HP a/b: at the heal floor, a cure is allowed
  if it leaves ... (#1065)`";
- "`# Fast-fragile in contact, HP a/b: even a full heal leaves ... no cure at
  the heal floor (#1065)`".

**One order of the exceptions** (review of #1089, P2). `contact_ban`
decides each contact for both callers:
1. bleeding out (`dot_kills`), or the floor with the ratio off: any cure;
2. then the cornered exception (`fast_fragile_no_heal = 2`): any cure;
3. then the ratio, at the floor, when a full heal passes it;
4. otherwise the ban holds.

`ban_of` folds the contacts (any ban bans; otherwise the heaviest ratio).
With the ratio on, `yield_to_cure` steps aside for a cure only when the
largest cure carried passes that same ban (`yield_allowed`). So attack on
sight never skips the attack for a cure the caution then refuses: in the
review's cornered case, 15/60 against 16 with only a CLW, both now allow
the CLW. `fast_fragile_no_heal` ships 1, so the cornered exception is off
in shipped play.

**A declined cure no longer costs the attack, and the caution keeps its
order** (re-reviews of #1089, P2). With the ratio on, attack on sight does
not drop its attack when it steps aside for a cure: it defers it. It
records the target and the think (`FightAttackState::ff_deferred`), and the
caution then runs in its usual order: cures, retreat, escapes, and the
outmatched heal at its original place. Only if the caution takes no action
does `fast_fragile_deferred_attack` make the priority attack, right after
`borg_caution` in `borg_think_dungeon`. It goes on the same target, if it
is still fast-fragile and safe to attack. The trace says "the caution took
no action for the cure; the priority attack on ... goes on (#1065)".

Example: a CL20 Borg at 15/60 with only a CSW, whose CSW gates refuse it.
It retreats or escapes if the caution would, and attacks otherwise.

An earlier fix at `d601343a` tried the cure at once instead, from attack on
sight. The re-review at `c7e55e1d` found that this drank the outmatched
heal before the caution's retreat and teleport checks, so it was replaced.
At 0 the yield stays a plain yield, as before.

**The healing fallbacks outside `borg_heal`** (re-review at `c7e55e1d`, P2).
These ask the contact ratio for each cure by its own heal (`ratio_gate`):
- the unseen-bump heal before the stairs (`unseen_bump_response`);
- the Recall wait (`buy_time_for_recall`, potions and the Rod of Healing);
- #1064's kill-on-sight floor cure (`kill_on_sight_floor_cure`), merged
  from `track-p` meanwhile.

The gate applies only when a fast-fragile attacker is in contact at the
heal floor; otherwise they pass, as before. #842's plain ban never covered
these fallbacks and is not extended to them here.

**When not even a full heal meets the ratio** (re-review at `47b46056`,
P2), the contact is `Contact::RatioClosed` and the ban
`HealBan::RatioClosed`. That is not #842's plain `Banned`, so the
fallbacks, which read only the ratio's verdicts (`fallback_ban`), refuse
every cure too, instead of passing as for #842's ban. Example: 5/11 HP
against 8 a turn at 30 needs 24 HP. `borg_heal` stops at either ban
(`blocks_all`). Test: `a_ratio_the_maximum_cannot_meet_closes_the_fallbacks_too`.

**The confusion and blindness fixes are an exception** (re-review of #1089,
P2). They come before the heal ban is read in `borg_heal`, as they did
before #842's ban, which never held them back. They cure what keeps the
Borg from reading Phase Door, so neither the ban nor its ratio stops them.
This is documented at the branch and pinned by
`the_status_cures_come_before_the_heal_ban`. That test also checks that
every potion, staff, rod, spell and activation call after the ban asks
`ok(...)`. Gating them instead would be the owner's call.

These cases keep their current behaviour:
- **Out of contact** (the crow at range in 592754909), the floor yields at
  any ratio, as the issue asks.
- **Bleeding out** still lifts the ban at any ratio: the cure that stops
  the bleeding is not a race.

With N = 0 the ban is `Free` or `Banned` exactly as `heal_banned` was, and
`yield_to_cure` does not read it, so play stays byte for byte the same.

The unit tests (`tests_1065`, at N = 30):
- **The issue's fatal points.** 10 to 17 max HP against 7.2 to 8.0 a turn:
  the ban holds. The Mage 645909552 (26 against 6.4): the cure is allowed,
  even from 2 HP. The Blackguard 2025089708 (60 against 10 to 14): a CLW at
  15 HP is allowed against 10 and not against 12; a CSW is allowed.
- **The reviews' cases.** Both potions (`each_cure_asks_the_ban_with_its_own_heal`);
  the Staff of Healing at 250/800
  (`every_healing_action_heals_what_the_game_data_says`); the cornered case
  through `contact_ban`, `ban_of` and `yield_allowed`
  (`caution_and_attack_on_sight_agree_on_the_cornered_exception`); the
  status-cure exception and the per-action gates after the ban
  (`the_status_cures_come_before_the_heal_ban`); the fallbacks' gates and
  the deferred attack's place in the think
  (`the_fallbacks_ask_the_ratio_and_the_attack_waits_for_the_caution`).
- **Minor Healing's amount.**
