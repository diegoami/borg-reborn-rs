## 2026-11-23: The fast-fragile heal floor in contact, by HP-to-damage ratio (#1065)

Every check below ran on the **default core, borg-rs** (`borg-core/`),
built from `origin/track-p` `8f32a8a0` plus this branch. No check ran on
the reborn core: this change does not touch it.

### What was done

One new parameter, `fast_fragile_heal_min_ratio` (tenths). The compiled
default and the shipped value in `harness/borg.txt` are both **0**, today's
play.

With N > 0 and the heal floor on (`fast_fragile_heal_floor` above 0), the
floor lifts the heal ban only if the cure buys turns. This applies **in
contact**, meaning an awake fast-fragile monster is adjacent. In both
places it acts, `heal_banned` (the caution's cures) and `yield_to_cure`
(attack on sight stepping aside), the floor then needs

    min(max HP, HP + heal) * 10 >= N * damage

- **heal** is the first cure carried, in the caution's order: Cure Light
  Wounds 20, then Serious 40, then Critical 60. These are exact amounts:
  `HEAL_HP` dice 20/40/60 with no percentage
  (`lib/gamedata/object.txt:3165-3166`, `:3184-3185`, `:3201-3202`), capped
  at max HP (`effect_handler_HEAL_HP`, `effect-handler-attack.c:201-232`).
- **damage** is the next turn's damage of the fast-fragile monsters within
  two grids: the mean, or the `fast_fragile_escape_quantile` quantile. It
  is the monsters' part of "the next turn kills" (`monsters_damage`,
  split out of `next_turn_damage`). The Borg's own cut and poison are left
  out, because the cure stops or shortens them.

Below the ratio the ban holds, and the escape (Phase Door, the stairs
underfoot) or the attack decides. The trace says so: "a cure leaves X HP
against Y damage/turn, Z turns, under N/10; no cure at the heal floor
(#1065)".

These cases keep their current behaviour:
- **Out of contact** (the crow at range in 592754909), the floor yields at
  any ratio, as the issue asks.
- **Bleeding out** (`fast_fragile_count_dot`, `dot_kills`) still lifts the
  ban at any ratio: the cure that stops the bleeding is not a race.
- The `fast_fragile_no_heal = 2` cornered exception is not touched.

With N = 0, `yield_to_cure` does not run its contact search, so play stays
byte for byte the same even with the floor on.

The issue's fatal points as unit tests (`tests_1065`, at N = 30):
- 10 to 17 max HP against 7.2 to 8.0 a turn: the ban holds.
- The Mage 645909552 (26 against 6.4): the cure is allowed, even from
  2 HP.
- The Blackguard 2025089708 (60 against 10 to 14): allowed at 35 HP after
  a CLW against 10. Not allowed against 12 (2.9 turns). Allowed after a
  CSW.

### The issue's third bullet (the rest), not done here

"A rest is never chosen below the floor while a fast-fragile monster was
seen within `fast_fragile_memory` turns (#982)." No such parameter exists.
#982 is open, and its PR #999 (`recover_rest_cure_hp_percent`, shipped 50
on that branch) already drinks instead of resting below a floor when a
fast awake monster is known within 20 grids. That covers 2125189042.
Adding a second rest gate here would duplicate it, so this PR leaves the
rest to #999.
