## 2026-11-27: Use the least nourishing food stack first (#1369)

The default Borg ranks known neutral food and eligible Remove Hunger
scrolls by nutrition per use times the number in that rucksack slot. Ties
favor fewer items to consume. The ranking uses the engine's effects and the
town planner's Hungry threshold for effects that raise food to a minimum.
Routine hunger, emergency eating and recovery all use the ranking.
Recovery ranks food even without a scroll and still tries its satisfy-hunger
activation if no stack qualifies. Spell priorities and scroll eligibility
follow the existing rules.

### What was found

The ranking covers neutral food and Remove Hunger scrolls. Waybread, the
Draught of the Ents and drinks with secondary effects keep their existing
fallback paths. Mushrooms and potions keep their own use rules. In routine
hunger, ranked food is now tried before the Slime Mold Juice fallback.

The regression test covers a single ration beating a larger stack of
biscuits, a missing or ineligible scroll, tied nutrition, and the exclusion
of empty, unknown and non-neutral stacks.

### What is left and owner decisions

The PR implements the stated total-nutrition rule. Minimizing the number of
actions to free a slot would be a separate policy decision. For example, two
apples total 20 nutrition and rank ahead of a single ration worth 30.
The PR is ready for owner acceptance after review and CI.
