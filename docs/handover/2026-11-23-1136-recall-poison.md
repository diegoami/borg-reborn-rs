## 2026-11-23: Flight Recall and the poison still to come (#1136)

**Done.** Two switches in the flight Recall forecast
(`flight_recall_survivable`, `recall_poison_hp`, borg-core/src/borg_caution.rs).
Both compile and ship **0**, and both need `caution_recall_blow_model = 1`:

- `caution_recall_poison_remaining` (proposal A). Already poisoned, the
  forecast charges the HP the running timer still takes plus the new poison,
  at most the delay. Today it charges the whole delay (34 HP).
  - The HP still to come is `ceil(timer / (adj_con_fix[CON] + 1))`: 1 HP a
    world tick (game-world.c:513-515), then the timer falls by
    `adj_con_fix + 1` (game-world.c:282, 317-320, 651).
  - The timer is read from the engine (`player_timed(TMD_POISONED)`). Owner,
    2026-11-23: "Engine counter", the same read the town planner already makes.
  - The CON index is `BI_CON_INDEX`, so there is no new boundary accessor.
  - `adj_con_fix` is copied from player-calcs.c:686-725; `tests_1136` pins
    the copy to the engine source.
- `caution_recall_poison_common` (proposal B, the simple form). The running
  poison is left out, because the timer falls the same whatever the Borg does.
  Only what new poison adds beyond it is charged: with the timer read,
  `min(delay, r + new) - min(delay, r)`; without it, the new poison alone (an
  upper bound). Cuts were never charged to Recall.

The forecast note names the new terms ("the running poison takes N more HP",
"the running poison left out").

**Found: the switches rescue none of the 25, and the issue's premise does not
hold.**

- In the base replays the 34-HP poison charge decided a refusal (the
  contact HP alone was below HP) only in three games: 727698927 (28
  refusals), 1575381233 (22) and 956247604 (8). In the other refusals the
  contact HP alone already reached HP.
- **727698927, 1575381233 and 1902932078 had no Recall to read.**
  - Each read its last Scroll of Word of Recall long before the fatal
    episode: t13915, t29120 and t27899 ("You have no more Scrolls of Word of
    Recall").
  - With the switches on, the forecast allows Recall at the decisive turns,
    `borg_recall` finds nothing to read, and the Borg goes on as before.
  - The "Refusing flight Recall" notes the issue counts come from
    `borg_recall_for_flight` (borg_escape.rs:102-107), which runs the
    forecast and logs its verdict before it asks whether there is anything
    to read.
- **956247604 had a scroll.**
  - With `common`, it read Recall at t87912 (HP 13, the running poison
    about 28 HP; `remaining` alone still refuses, correctly), rested for
    Recall, and died of poison at t88060 before the Recall fired.
  - Refusing was not what killed it: the poison would have killed it
    whatever it did. That is the case `common` is designed for.
