# Compare carried melee alternatives and clear duplicate Home weapons

The exploration-first spare check now compares known melee weapons with worn
and carried alternatives. It checks attack rating, blows, normal and
slay/brand damage, weight, speed, armour, and known equipment benefits. A
specialist's ordinary damage matters when it can improve on the primary weapon.
The check uses standard combat damage before criticals and preserves weapons
with unknown properties, the selected swap and the digger. The ordinary
melee comparison leaves artifacts and activating equipment eligible for Home. Home
uses the same comparison with a per-blow check for future loadouts, and its
existing disposal and reserve planner decides what to remove or store.

Reloading A1 on the original build reached town with all nine melee spares
and no carried devices. Home was full, with seven melee weapons, one sling and no
wands, rods or staves. Reloading the same checkpoint with the change drops the
1d4 Dragon Dagger, Dragon Whip, Troll Whip and Frost Main Gauche under pack
pressure. The first town inventory includes a Rod of Light, Wand of Magic
Missile, and Staff of Mapping. Home releases the Lightning Main Gauche, Orc
Rapier and Flame Tulwar. A1 stashes the *Slay Dragon* Tulwar and later still
has the digging shovel available at Home.

- The 1d6 Dragon Dagger is the light, more accurate dragon swap. The Tulwar
  offers the stronger dragon multiplier and Constitution as a future option.
- Rilia covers the ordinary Troll Whip. The *Slay Troll* Spear adds Strength.
- Nimthanc covers the weaker Frost Main Gauche in the dungeon. Once in town,
  all three elemental daggers are compared with the actual worn resistances.
  Rilia and the rest of A1's gear make one backup sufficient.
- The spare Sling of Power trades accuracy for damage, and the shovel has a
  distinct digging role.

A2 starts with a full Home and carries Dethanc and Nimthanc. His worn robe
and shield supply the lightning and cold resistances. Home already holds
Narthanc, whose base kind is also Dagger. The three daggers have identical
melee stats and different single-element resistances, brands and bolt
activations. A2's worn gear already covers the resistances, so one backup
at Home suffices. A dagger that fills an uncovered resistance can still
stay available. In a tie the planner keeps the one already at Home, and
uses the normal Home withdrawal and disposal path for redundant stock.
The original A2 checkpoint keeps Narthanc and sells the other two from the
pack. A checkpoint continued to turn 4,147,450 with all three already at
Home withdraws and sells Narthanc and Nimthanc, leaving Dethanc.
The saved quiver holds 59 matching pebbles across four stacks. All have
positive combat bonuses, which protect them from the existing excess-ammo
cleanup.

Home aims for one third scarce consumables and useful devices, with gear
using most of the remaining space. This affects replacement choices and
leaves any Home slot available to useful stock. The first stack of Healing,
Speed, Restore Life Levels, safe stat-restoring potions and Restore Mana for
casters can claim a slot from weaker spares. Actual upgrades and artifacts
stay protected during reserve replacement when they fill a distinct role.
Older town records retain their
planner rules.

Home now compares armour in the same slot. A piece can leave when a lighter
or equally heavy alternative has at least as much protection and the worn
kit covers any missing resistance. Launchers are compared against matching
subtypes in the pack, equipment or Home. A move of one variant to Home
keeps the disposal route available for another, clearly inferior variant.

A3 starts with ten slings in the pack and one weaker sling in Home. The
replay drops eight slings in the dungeon and clears the weaker Home sling.
The two Slings of Accuracy trade accuracy for damage. The Mage's replay
clears the plain and inferior Studded Leather Armour, three weaker pairs
of Iron Shod Boots and a spare sling. It keeps the Potion of Healing and
the strongest pair of boots available.

The checksum-pinned [A1, A2 and A3 fixtures](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-reborn-rs/issues/1269)
use the default borg-rs core with the exact saved settings.
Run `python3 engine/tests/check_weapon_hoard.py A1 /path/to/extracted-a1`
and repeat with `A2` and `A3` and their extracted fixtures. Use a normal
harness build. The public exploration-pack fixture covers peer
comparison, a stronger slay and a unique activation. The Home tests cover
same-kind artifact moves, triage for a full inventory and the soft reserve
share.
