## 2026-11-19: the owner's Phase Door rule, behind a switch (#584, step 2)

Owner: "to use Phase Door, enemies must be on the square next to you";
otherwise attack with magic or missiles, and if not possible boost (Speed
included); only in close quarters, when the Borg thinks it cannot win, Phase
Door. Built on step 1's one decision point (#602, `phase_door`).

- **`escape_phase_adjacent_rule`** (flag, compiled 0, shipped 0), applied in
  `phase_door` before every site's own check (`adjacent_rule`,
  `phase_verdict`):
  - no awake enemy adjacent: no Phase Door; the best attack by the Borg's
    own ranking (`borg_attack`; with nothing adjacent only a ranged one
    can win), else, with an awake monster in view, a boost
    (`cornered_boost`, made `pub(crate)`: Speed first);
  - adjacent, fight not lost: counter-attack, else the boost;
  - adjacent and lost (danger ≥ `escape_phase_lost_danger_percent`, 100, of
    the current HP): the site's own check and Phase Door as before.
- **The stuck (twitchy) phases** follow the rule too: with no enemy
  adjacent there is no Phase Door to get unstuck.
- docs/PHASE-DOOR.md has the rule's table; `harness/borg.txt` writes both
  settings out at their compiled values.
