## Weight-5 stair-flight defaults for all classes (#786)

The owner selected the uniform weight-5 profile and requested integration
with refreshed shipped smoke references. The six settings live in the
shared `harness/borg.txt`. The clean-path blocker threshold is 50% of
current HP. Ranged-first and the rising HP override ship at 0.
[Issue #786](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-reborn-rs/issues/786)

The weight-5 local comparison is directional. Relative to mode 3, Ranger
deaths went from 12 to 5 in 200 starts and the other classes went from 4
to 5 in 400 starts. Two initial wall timeouts reached the turn cap on
retry. The Zoo pilot has its own reviewed specimen and profiles.
