## 2026-11-28: No store re-entry loop at a frozen game turn (#1395)

### What was done

- **Trace.** When the live town planner leaves a store with no transaction
  queued, the core trace (`trace-core.jsonl`) records a `store_exit`: the
  store, why, and the goal and action still planned. Each path that leaves
  without a transaction names its reason (`borg_town_live::why`): the town
  visit's time or bounce limit, an arrival unload or quiver trim waiting
  outside, the arrival sale done, an action for another store, the ware
  gone, the carrying preflight, a transaction not queued, an action with no
  store, no stores. The field is optional, so `core-trace@2` readers keep
  working; nothing is added to `messages.log`.
- **Hold.** That exit records an idle visit: the store, the game turn and
  what the Borg carries (gold and, per slot, kind, artifact and copies).
  While the game turn and the carried state are the same, the store's offers
  count as rejected (`buy_rejected`, `sale_rejected`), so the planner chooses
  another store's action. A destination kept from before (`choose` keeps a
  destination while walking) is given up and replanned at once, with a note
  `# Town plan: store N did nothing at this game turn; not entering it again
  until something changes (#1395)`. Once the turn moves or the pack or purse
  changes, the store is planned again. A visit that queued a transaction
  records nothing, so a follow-up in the same visit is untouched.
- **Regression.** `engine/tests/test_store_loop.py` (in CI after
  `TownSpareSaleTest`) runs the ranked-home town case. With
  `BORG_TEST_REFUSE_STORE=0` (feature `test-town-review`) the General Store
  refuses every transaction and keeps the destination, as the #1395
  Warrior's store handler did: the store is entered at most once per game
  turn (177 times at turn 141 without the hold) and the trace names the
  refusal. Without it, no hold happens and the two General Store purchases
  at turn 4400 (a follow-up in one visit) remain. Unit tests cover the
  carried-state match.

### What was found

- Entering with `5` and leaving cost no game time:
  cmd-cave.c:do_cmd_hold sets `energy_use = 0` after the store UI, over the
  exit's move (ui-store.c:1305). Only the Borg's own limits ended the loop.
- A store shows the same item with another description, and an empty slot
  can keep a stale kind, so the hold compares kinds and copies, not
  descriptions (the first version never matched).
- The incident does not reproduce from the turn-266801 save on the
  recording build (`cae077d`) or on `track-p`: a loaded save loses the
  Borg's memory, and the replay runs ~20 turns ahead of the live game before
  the loop. On both builds it reaches the same Home-door drops; on `track-p`
  they step to floor and are ignored (#1397). The live trace still does not
  say which exit fired; the new `store_exit` record would.
- With a store that refuses forever (the test hook), the Borg now goes back
  and forth between Home and that store, spending game time, until the
  planner's existing town limits (2000 thinks, 1350 bounces). That is not a
  frozen turn, but it is wasted time.

### What is left, and owner decisions

- **Hold scope (owner).** The hold lasts for the game turn, as #1395 asks.
  Holding the store for the rest of the visit while the carried state is
  unchanged would also stop the back-and-forth above, at the cost of
  missing a need that grows with time alone (light or food running down).
- The C chooser (`borg_town_plan = 0`) is not covered.
- The replay comparison is gameplay evidence for the #1395 case in
  borg-cemetery (PR 166); not recorded there yet.
