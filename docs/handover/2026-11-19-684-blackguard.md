their spells?", then "record the findings, but we need different behaviour

  Not shipped.

  0 SP; castable with 2+ enemies adjacent once in 1,219. The game's mana
  design, not a missed choice.
- **The zoo deaths do not reproduce on main 826cde4c** (1 of 40 died).

    hitting (general, #532; a Blackguard has the HP for it);
