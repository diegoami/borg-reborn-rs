## 2026-11-24: Trial an HP margin for the priority fight (#992)

The standalone experiment adds `caution_winning_fight_margin_percent`.
It compiles and ships 0. An enabled trial asks the priority fight to
leave strictly more than the configured share of current HP after
expected damage. Use 20 as a hypothesis. A rejection yields to ordinary
caution. The attack-on-sight and lone-target paths have their own gates.
Phase Door keeps the original race verdict. Diagnostic mode 2 reports a
trial rejection alongside the original verdict and takes no priority attack.

On Track P `3c46575d`, Human Priest `1512540769` first diverges at turn
56,636. The control swings its mace at Fang. With 20 the estimated
2.835 HP left at 27 HP fails the check and caution takes the first
step of a two-step route to a down staircase. Both reach the 60,000-turn
cap at CL5 and max DL3. The control matches the audited head's key
prefix. A unit test also pins the historical Elf Paladin's 0.5–1.5 HP
margins and the strict boundary at the configured share.

In a fresh resume of the Hobbit Rogue checkpoint, the two arms reached
turn 1,554,800 at the same position, HP, and RNG. At 12/83 HP with 1.978
expected HP left against a skeleton human, the 20% arm yielded to caution
and drank Cure Serious Wounds, reaching 52 HP. The zero-margin arm used
the priority attack. This is separate from the original watched game's
fatal zombified-orc turn, where a manually selected Cure Light Wounds
saved the Rogue in the matched replay.
