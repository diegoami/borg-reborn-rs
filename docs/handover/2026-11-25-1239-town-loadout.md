# Town shopping and departure loadout (#1239)

The live town planner prices purchases and Home withdrawals by utility, gold,
and pack slots while the Borg is overloaded. Plan record v21 keeps the old
weight restrictions when replaying earlier schemas. A live departure pass
keeps urgent purchases available, sheds only surplus above the trip reserves,
and prevents optional purchases from reopening the shopping pass.

Departure keeps the existing plain-ammo trim first. If the Borg still needs
headroom, it tries known lighter weapons and armour with the old piece
removed from the carried kit. It compares effective speed, carry room, and
equipment power, while preserving resistance, combat and spell capability,
and critical supplies. A replacement from a full Home can make its own
vacancy. Otherwise the planner can clear rejected stock, common consumables,
or redundant gear to fit the displaced item. Each equipment slot is tried
once per visit. Confirmed deposits keep the heavy gear at Home for
reconsideration when a later town visit can accommodate it. The Borg logs
remaining overload when it exhausts the safe options.

The captured save referenced in [#1239](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-reborn-rs/issues/1239)
was unavailable in this workspace. Rust regressions use its reported carry
deficit, purchase options, and equipment weights. The broader Home stock
policy remains in [#1241](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-reborn-rs/issues/1241),
and per-class quiver reserve calibration remains in
[#1199](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-reborn-rs/issues/1199).
