## 2026-11-28: Runner contention with borg-zoo measured; `ci.yml` left alone (#1437)

### What was done

- `scripts/ci-contention.py` (with `scripts/tests/test_ci_contention.py`)
  measures the question the issue asks, read only, from the GitHub API: the
  wait of every job of this repository's `ci.yml` and of borg-zoo's
  `compare.yml` (`created_at` to `started_at`), the zoo's waits split by
  whether a job of ours was queued or running when the zoo job was created,
  and an attribution of every zoo wait over 30 s to what held the 20 shared
  slots at that moment (**self**: the zoo's own running jobs filled them;
  **reborn**: the zoo alone would have fitted, its jobs plus ours did not;
  **other**: neither side filled them). Re-run it with
  `python3 scripts/ci-contention.py --days 7` once the zoo dispatches again
  (borg-zoo#827's automatic pilots do), or `--since/--until` for a window.
- `ci.yml` is **not** changed: there is nothing yet that shows our runs delay
  the zoo's.

### What was found

The zoo has dispatched nothing since 2026-11-27T04:58, so there is no data
after #1418–#1421 (about 8 runner jobs per run) and none for the pilots. The
history before it, 2026-11-23 to 25, when the zoo ran 1,594 `compare` jobs and
we ran 1,793 CI jobs (up to 23 of ours queued or running at one moment):

| | jobs | median wait | p90 | longest |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| borg-reborn-rs `ci.yml` | 1,793 | 3 s | 15 s | 589 s |
| borg-zoo `compare.yml` | 1,594 | 3 s | 841 s | 3,004 s |

Our CI was in flight for 85 % of the zoo's jobs, so that split alone says
nothing (p90 841 s with it, 822 s without). The attribution does: of 529 zoo
jobs that waited over 30 s,

| held the slots | zoo jobs | job-hours waited |
|---|---|---|
| the zoo's own jobs (a run's matrix queueing behind itself) | 466 | 96.8 |
| the zoo alone would have fitted; our jobs filled the rest | 11 | 0.4 |
| neither side (other workflows or repositories of the account) | 52 | 10.3 |

So 90 % of the zoo's waiting is the zoo's own, and 0.3 % is ours. The longest
waits of our jobs (589 s) are the reverse: the zoo holding the slots. On
2026-11-26 to 27 (the zoo at 90 jobs) no job of either side waited more than
18 s.

### Decision rule, and the change to apply if it bites

Apply it when a window with the zoo busy shows the **reborn** line above
about 5 % of the zoo's delayed job-hours, or the zoo's waits while our CI ran
a median over a minute. Then, in `.github/workflows/ci.yml`, give `track-p`
push runs one cancelling group per branch, and leave `main`'s as they are:

```yaml
concurrency:
  group: ci-${{ github.event_name == 'pull_request' && format('pr-{0}', github.event.pull_request.number)
    || github.ref == 'refs/heads/track-p' && 'push-track-p'
    || format('run-{0}', github.run_id) }}
  cancel-in-progress: ${{ github.event_name == 'pull_request' || github.ref == 'refs/heads/track-p' }}
```

(and update the comment above it). The price: the tree after an intermediate
merge is then checked only by its pull request's run, the newest head still
fully; with no merge queue on `track-p` (#1423) the push run is today the
only check of a merged tree. The alternative is `strategy.max-parallel` on
the three full-suite shards.
