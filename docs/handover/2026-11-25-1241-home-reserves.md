## 2026-11-25 — Home reserves (#1241)

The live planner can clear common surplus and inferior spare equipment below
the existing per-kind stack cap. It retains a common reserve that still serves
the next kit, and it considers rechargeable devices and alternative equipment
as future stock. A planned reserve purchase uses the ordinary purse, the
Black Market allowance, and native carry and Home space. It goes to Home only
after the engine confirms checkout. Ordinary dungeon cleanup holds onto useful
finds while emergency pack relief remains available.

The known cursed sling uses the ordinary withdrawal and discard path, including
when no store accepts it. A wearable improvement can be withdrawn when Home is
full if it fits in the pack.

`borg_param_town_home_reserve = 1` enables the policy in the shipped profile
at the owner's direction. Home cleanup keeps copies needed by weighted
requirement rows even if a spell supplies the same effect. Light and Blessing
have no such row and can be cleared whole when a spell covers them. Heroism
and Boldness can feed the prepared fear reserve and can be cleared whole
when their applicable requirements are covered. For a cooling rod, the
planner uses the engine's average recharge time and aggregate `timeout`
to check whether at least one charge will be ready during the trip
(`engine/angband/src/obj-util.c`, `number_charging` and `recharge_timeout`).

The owner follow-up gives useful rare equipment first claim on Home slots.
A lighter viable alternative moves ahead of heavier gear when carrying
capacity is tight. Scarce consumables and reliable devices come next, with
the capability model valuing their uses, including speed, temporary
resistances, healing and escape. A useful intermittent normal-shop spare
can take an empty slot and is a candidate for eviction when a higher
priority item needs room. Readily replenished stock stays out of Home
deposits and is cleared down to explicit supply floors. The stock frequency
comes from the engine's `always` and `normal` shop catalogues. The owner
specifically wants Staff of Teleportation treated as a scarce escape reserve
and Remove Curse treated as an expendable spare.
The lighter-loadout pass uses the same priorities when it needs to free a
slot for the gear it displaces. Explicit exclusions take precedence over
effect-based reserve scoring for both town cleanup and loadout eviction.
For example, Second Sight's effects count in the capability model, but the
Borg has no action to use the mushroom, so Home treats it as rejected stock.
