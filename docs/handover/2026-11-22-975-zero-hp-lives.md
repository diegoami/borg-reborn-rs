**Done.** New parameter `kill_live_zero_hp` (flag, compiled 0, not shipped):

exactly 0 HP. `live_power` in `borg-core/src/borg_flow_kill.rs:386-406`,

**Found** (every check on the default core, borg-rs, built from track-p
`4b2c2fcf` plus this change; no reborn-core run).

- *No message lowers the estimate.* `HIT:` / `PAIN:` only locate the monster
  (`borg_update.rs:1749`, `:1801`). `kill.power` is cheated from the engine
  monster on every update (`set_health`, `borg_flow_kill.rs:375`):
  `maxhp * (100*hp/maxhp) / 100`, then #546's floor, which applies only to
  `hp > 0`.
- *Cause.* The engine kills a monster only at `hp < 0` (`mon_take_hit`,
  `engine/angband/src/mon-util.c:1300`). A 2-HP grey mold hit for 2 (an
  arrow, 1d4) or a 3-HP grey mushroom patch hit for 3 (Main Gauche, 1d5)
  lives at 0 HP and reads 0. The pain message agrees: "jerks limply" is pain
  type 2's last message (`pain.txt:37`), MON_MSG_0, newhp*100/oldhp ≤ 10
  (`mon-msg.c:105-114`). The focused fight planner (#759) already counts
  hp + 1 (`observed_kill_hp`, `borg_fight_attack.rs:5277`, "needs 1 HP").
- *What reads the 0.* The overkill caps (`borg_fight_attack.rs:694` melee,
  `:1629` ranged) cap damage at `power × multiplier` = 0, so every
  candidate of `borg_attack` scores 0 ("nonpositive_return" in the trace).
  Also `kill_fraction`, the "attack kills" bonus (`:1829`), the partial-kill
  danger divisions (`:1813`, `:4150`), the bolt-sleep test (`:1798`) and
  crush (`borg_danger.rs:2574`). Added after the review of #994: the
  shoot-n-scoot gate (`borg_shoot_scoot_safe`, `borg_flow_kill.rs:3176`,
  `kill.power >= CL`), where the flag works the other way: at CL1 a 0-HP
  monster now counts as an adjacent monster worth a Phase Door (only an
  awake one that moves, so never a mold or a mushroom patch); the reserve
  spell's HP test (`borg_fight_attack.rs:3265`, unchanged at floor 1 and
  margin 4, but a floor near 10 would skip the spell); the Unlife tap
  (`:1583`, 0 damage today, 1 with the flag); the vampire wake test
  (`:4137`). `borg_caution.rs:2102` and `borg_escape.rs:1099` read it too and
  do not change (injury is 100 at hp 0).
- *The danger-0 inference is refuted.* In `borg_danger` `kill.power` enters
  only breath damage (`borg_danger.rs:815`) and crush; a grey mold has
  neither. The trace's danger 0 at 5454 is the danger on the Borg's own
  square before the step (16 after it), and `borg_flow_old`'s `select_step`
  (`borg_flow.rs:1704`) checks no danger. What 0 HP did: at think 579
  (turn 5454) `BF_LAUNCH` scored 0, so no second arrow; the flow continued
  next to the mold.
