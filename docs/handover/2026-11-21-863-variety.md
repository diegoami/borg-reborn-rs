
## 2026-11-21: variety in the backpack, prepared remedies (#863)

Owner: "Better 4 cure poison, 4 detect invisible and 3 remove curse in the
pack and 20 light wounds in the pack than 40 light wound potions"; the point
is being prepared to a variety of situations, a copy is worth only while it
is likely to be used on the trip. The owner also said the valuation of the
other objects needs a redesign, not a per-curve patch.

Done, on `task/T863-variety`, **stacked on #864** (`ea65baac`), first PR of
two (potions and scrolls):

- `town_prepared_percent` (compiled 0, **shipped 8**) and
  `town_prepared_poison`, `_invisible`, `_curse`, `_confusion`, `_fear`
  (shipped 3, 4, 2, 2, 2; the owner: "if I have 20 CLW I would have say 4
  detect invisible and 2 remove curse"): five new capability rows, one per
  situation's remedy, each a requirement on the trip weight: each of the
  first N copies is worth the percent of the trip weight, nothing past N.
  They sit on top of the condition-present curves, which are unchanged. The
  first copies of each remedy beat the 21st Cure Light Wounds. The planner
  buys them by value per gold after the urgent needs and the staples, so a
  richer Borg buys more of the dearer ones (Remove Curse is 100 gold,
  Detect Invisible 15). Per class through `@<class>`. Before this, the Borg
  never bought Detect Invisible or Remove Curse.
- Staples that lose value (owner: "make staple objects start losing value";
  "it must be a decaying curve"; "Food and Light start decaying from 4, phase
  door, flasks and CLW from 6, CSW from 4, CCW 2, ... class dependent";
  "extrapolate values from what classes buy now ... somewhat similar first,
  but then leaning on more variety"). `town_staple_clw`, `_csw`, `_ccw`,
  `_phase`, `_oil`, `_food`, `_light` are the free copies of each (0 = no
  decay), `town_staple_decay_percent` (80) the share each later copy keeps of
  the copy before. Each of the three healing potions has its own row on the
  shared HEAL curve; Phase Door, oil, food and (without a lantern) torches
  one each. **Shipped on**: common 6, 4, 2, 6 (Phase Door, class values above it), 6 (oil, class values), 4, 4, and per class (the
  class files, folded into `harness/borg.txt`) Cure Light, Cure Serious,
  Phase Door and oil, scaled from what 36 games (9 classes, 4 seeds, 200,000
  game turns) carried at a town departure before this change; the table and
  the rule are in `docs/CALIBRATION.md`. `smoke-borg-rs` is re-recorded.
  The owner's "object value file with class overrides" is the existing
  parameters and class files; no new file format.
- Owner, 2026-11-21, on the baseline: "current characters do not buy enough
  world of recall and should buy more, and probably more phase doors as well,
  they tend to run out of them, especially rangers", and "it is important
  that in the first level we have the staples". Shipped: `town_recall_reserve`
  2, `buy_recall_from_gold` 200 (was 400), `town_phase_reserve` 4 (Ranger and
  Blackguard 8, Priest 6; the Ranger's was 2), `town_variety_min_depth` 2
  (the decay and the remedies only start after the Borg has been below the
  first level); the Phase Door and oil free copies of the staples are above
  what each class carries now, near what it uses. In 36 games Phase Door
  departures with 5 or fewer drop for every class that had them (Priest 64 to
  18 percent, Blackguard 50 to 0) and the Phase Doors bought a game rise
  (Rogue 3.5 to 9.0, Blackguard 3.8 to 14.5); Recall improves for the
  casters, Rogue and Ranger (the share of departures with none: Necromancer
  100 to 40 percent, Priest 62 to 33, Ranger 68 to 41) and not for Warrior,
  Paladin and Blackguard (39 to 57, 31 to 71, 75 to 85); four starts a
  class. Missiles and Word of Recall are not touched by the staples' decay.

- **The review of #873** (MiniMax-M3, at `05bcc358`) found three things, all
  fixed: F1 (blocking) `Model::mask` was 64 bits and folded every capability id
  from 63 up onto one bit, so a fear remedy and a poison remedy, or a healing
  potion and a Word of Recall, were one group and the solver chose one option for
  all of them; the mask is 128 bits, bit 63 stays the packing-room sentinel, and
  `every_capability_has_its_own_mask_bit` and `different_remedies_are_different_groups`
  fail on the old code. F2 the knee unpacking is a named `Knee` whose per-copy
  unit is documented per row kind, with a test of the healing rows. F3 the
  `town_prepared_percent` comment in `harness/borg.txt` says a target. My
  CALIBRATION sentence that the merge "only widens the search" was wrong and is
  replaced. `track-p` (with #864 merged) is merged into the branch. The local
  36-game numbers are re-measured on the fixed build against a control on the
  same build (CALIBRATION): Phase Door is not better in every class.
