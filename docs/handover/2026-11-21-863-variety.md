
## 2026-11-21: variety in the backpack, prepared remedies (#863)

Owner: "Better 4 cure poison, 4 detect invisible and 3 remove curse in the
pack and 20 light wounds in the pack than 40 light wound potions"; the point
is being prepared to a variety of situations, a copy is worth only while it
is likely to be used on the trip. The owner also said the valuation of the
other objects needs a redesign, not a per-curve patch.

Done, on `task/T863-variety`, **stacked on #864** (`ea65baac`), first PR of
two (potions and scrolls):

- `town_prepared_percent` (compiled and shipped 0) and `town_prepared_copies`
  (2): above 0, five new capability rows, one per situation's remedy (poison,
  curse, invisible, confusion, fear), each a small diminishing curve on the
  trip weight, fed one copy per supplying item (`supplies_of_kind`). They sit
  on top of the condition-present curves, which are unchanged. The first
  copies of each remedy beat the 21st Cure Light Wounds; past a couple each
  is worth little. Per class through `@<class>`.
- `town_staple_free_copies` (0 = off) and `town_staple_past_percent` (25),
  the owner's "make staple objects start losing value starting from the 8th,
  compared to the objects that are not in the starter's shopping list":
  healing potions (a copy counted as a Cure Light Wounds) and Phase Door
  keep the percent of their marginal value after the free copies, carried
  ones counted. Suggested pilot: 7 free copies, 25 percent. Light, food, oil
  and arrows have caps of their own and are not changed; "the starter's
  staples" is my reading: the healing potions and Phase Door.
- A bug of the first commit, fixed in the second: `observe` set the
  `RECALL_RESERVE` row when the model did not have it (Recall and prepared
  both off, the shipped state of those two) and would have panicked in a live
  game; the unit tests don't call `observe`. The model keeps its length when
  neither new feature is on.
- `Model::mask` caps a capability id at bit 63 (ids now reach 68).
- `docs/CALIBRATION.md`, section "#863".

Found: Cure Light Wounds does not cure poison here (checked in `object.txt`);
the planner's own poison count already ignores it. Surplus-first storing
(#864's `town_store_keep_min`) already protects variety when weight forces
storing, and `town_max_non_missile_copies` (35) is an existing hard cap on
buying copies.

Not done, and not checked:

- Wands, staves and rods outside `WORTHLESS`, and keeping a found device
  rather than selling it (the second PR of #863).
- Remove Hunger and the Cure Critical Wounds stack: Remove Hunger is in the
  food floor already; CCW feeds the poison row through its CURE:POISONED.
- No step-level test of a pack buying or storing. Engine checks: the smoke
  suite at the shipped settings is 3/3 identical to `smoke-borg-rs` (the
  switches are shipped 0, the lines are added to `harness/borg.txt`), and a
  60,000-turn game (seed 101, Human Warrior) with each switch on runs the
  town plan without a panic and plays differently from the one with both
  off. That is not a measurement of what the Borg carries.
- No zoo pilot (RULES 20). The zoo request is: shipped against
  `town_prepared_percent` 25 and 50 with copies 2 and 3; kinds carried at
  departure and situations covered, deaths by cause (poison, invisible
  monsters, curses), CL and depth.
- `scripts/borg-core-vendor.py check` needs `vendor/borg-rs`, absent here.
