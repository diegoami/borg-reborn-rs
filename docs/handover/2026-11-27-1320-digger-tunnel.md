# A digger is valued as the tunnelling swap by its digging power

Issue #1320, found while writing #1315's native test. The owner chose the
first option in the issue: read the real digging power, so a digger is
carried as the tunnelling swap from max depth 25 to 40 as the code intends.

## Found

- `weapon_value` (`borg_trait_swap.rs`) adds `digger * 3500 + 1000` for
  max depth 25–40 when gold is under 100,000. `borg_notice_weapon_swap`
  set `digger` from the item's `pval`, as the C Borg does
  (`borg-trait-swap.c:274`).
- 4.2 diggers have `pval` 0. The engine counts their digging as the
  DIG_1/2/3 level plus the TUNNEL modifier (`player-calcs.c:1959-1969`).
  The tunnelling reward was therefore dead code: a digger was the swap only
  by its melee value. A plain Shovel at max depth 30 had swap value -1.

## Done

- `digger_power(item)` in `borg_trait_swap.rs` returns the DIG level plus
  the item's known `OBJ_MOD_TUNNEL` modifier, the engine's sum.
- New flag `swap_digger_tunnel`.
  - Default 0 keeps the C `pval` reading.
  - It ships at 1 in `harness/pborg.yaml`.
  - The other digger gates are unchanged: a digger is not noticed while the
    wielded weapon digs or while Stone to Mud is castable, and a worthless
    or cursed one is skipped.
- `docs/CALIBRATION.md`: a new section and its parameter row. The #1315
  section no longer says the bonus is dead.
- `scripts/borg-core-vendor.py`: the `HOOKED` note for
  `borg_trait_swap.rs` names the new parameter.

## Checked

- Unit test `digger_power_is_the_dig_level_plus_the_tunnel_modifier`:
  - a Shovel (pval 0) is 1, and 4 with a +3 tunnel modifier;
  - a Mattock is 3;
  - a non-digger is 0.
- Native case `digger-swap`: the town Priest carries a plain Shovel at max
  depth 30.
  - At 1 the Shovel is the weapon swap, with `digger` 1 and a value above
    4,500.
  - At 0 there is no swap: value -1 and `digger` 0.

  Both runs are in `engine/tests/test_town_loadout.py`, and each checks
  that its branch ran.
- The shipped smoke suite is 4 of 4 identical to `smoke-borg-rs`, so no
  re-record is needed.
- The four cargo commands pass, as do the town loadout, exploration pack
  and recall loadout native tests, and the citation check.

## Not checked

  measurement, and the shipped 1 is a hypothesis until then.
- Whether the Borg wields the swap to tunnel when it should. That is the
  digging code's business; this change only decides what is carried.
