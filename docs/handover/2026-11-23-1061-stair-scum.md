## 2026-11-23: stair_scum, an exploration mode under 25 max HP (#1061)

Track P, P8. Branch `claude/wizardly-hamilton-ujijf5` (the session's assigned
branch, not `task/...`), from `origin/track-p` `8f32a8a0`.

### Done

- **`borg-core/src/borg_stair_scum.rs`** (new, ours): the owner's mode, behind
  `stair_scum` (shipped 0). It applies in the dungeon while max HP <
  `stair_scum_max_hp` (per class) + `stair_scum_max_hp_race_delta` (per race)
  and the Borg cannot detect monsters (Detect Monsters, Reveal Monsters or
  Detection castable, or a Rod of Detection carried). The issue's six points:
  1. the safe radius *R(g)* = *D(g)* / (`stair_scum_speed_ratio` − 1) + HP /
     `stair_scum_contact_damage`, against *s(g)*, the walk over known grids to
     the nearest known staircase of either kind (its own BFS, not
     `borg_flow_cost_stair`, which measures one up staircase, #1062);
  2. the dark flows keep only the candidates inside the radius, nearest the
     staircase first; items (`take_select`, `scum_select`) and monster hunts
     (`borg_flow_kill`) outside the radius are skipped; outside its radius the
     Borg walks back onto a staircase, right after caution;
  3. a door it opens: a step back toward the staircase (a wait, on it), a
     turn's wait, then on; "You hear a door burst open!" or a known closed
     door seen open inside the radius holds it on a staircase for
     `stair_scum_alarm_turns` game turns;
  4. nothing left inside the radius: it takes a staircase (DL1: down when
     prepared for DL2, else up; deeper: up first);
  5. on a staircase it rests there, and a threat the fight race did not take
     is left by the stairs (`leave_stairs_on_monster`);
  6. the mode ends at the threshold; C's leash is never removed.
- **A race suffix for every parameter** (`params.rs`): `__<race>` / `@<race>`
  and `BORG_PARAM_<NAME>__<RACE>`, the `p_race.txt` names in lower case with
  `-` as `_`. Class value, then race value, then the common one;
  `params-core.json` gives `race_values` only where set; a unit test checks no
  race name is a class name. docs/ZOO-INTERFACE.md, "Race values".
- 6 parameters (registry 2421), each in `harness/borg.txt` at its default and
  in docs/CALIBRATION.md, "stair_scum (#1061)". `engine.rs` gains
  `race_ridx_if_set` (read only, beside `class_cidx_if_set`).

### Found

- On DL1 at CL1–3 the torch radius is about 3 steps, so a level is used up in
  a few turns, and the plan allows DL1 only: the Borg goes up to town and
  straight down to a new DL1, many times (the smoke Warrior: 17 level
  changes by the mode in 20,000 turns). The town leg runs C's town code (scary town
  monsters, departure checks). This is the mode's own cost, as the issue says
  ("XP will come mostly from what wanders to the staircase").
- C's DL1 "grab an item before going back to town" (`borg_think_stair_scum`,
  before caution) walked 7 steps out; the radius now filters it too.
- The walk to a lit-room target can cross a dark corridor whose own radius
  is smaller; turning back there would repeat for ever. The return compares
  with the widest *D* of a target admitted since the Borg last stood on a
  staircase.
