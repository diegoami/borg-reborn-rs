## 2026-11-23: stair_scum, an exploration mode under 25 max HP (#1061)

Track P, P8. Branch `claude/wizardly-hamilton-ujijf5` (the session's assigned
branch, not `task/...`), from `origin/track-p` `8f32a8a0`. The design was
reworked after Codex's design review on #1061 (comment 7088771219); the
findings and what answers them are under "Review".

### Done

- **`borg-core/src/borg_stair_scum.rs`** (new, ours): the owner's mode, behind
  `stair_scum` (shipped 0). It applies in the dungeon while max HP <
  `stair_scum_max_hp` (per class) + `stair_scum_max_hp_race_delta` (per race)
  and the Borg cannot detect monsters (Detect Monsters, Reveal Monsters or
  Detection castable, or a Rod of Detection carried). The issue's six points:
  1. **safe grids**: a grid is safe when a monster first seen there, acting
     `stair_scum_speed_ratio` times per player action (and `ratio - 1` times
     before the Borg's first), lands fewer than HP /
     `stair_scum_contact_damage` attacks while the Borg walks to the nearest
     known staircase of either kind and takes it. The walk is over known,
     trap-free floor and doors (its own BFS, not `borg_flow_cost_stair`, which
     measures one up staircase, #1062). The first-sight distance follows the
     engine's light (`cur_light - 1`; unlight), a lit room's nearest unseen
     entry, and 1 behind a door;
  2. **the safe area** (safe grids joined to a staircase through safe grids):
     dark-flow candidates in it, nearest the staircase first; items
     (`take_select`, `scum_select`), veins (`borg_flow_vein`) and monster
     hunts (`borg_flow_kill`) outside it skipped; every voluntary step of
     `borg_flow_old` that leaves it without coming nearer the staircase
     refused (flights and the leaving walk excepted); outside it the Borg
     walks back onto a staircase, right after caution;
  3. a door it opens: a step back toward the staircase (a wait, on it), a
     turn's wait, then on; a known closed door seen open next to the area
     holds it on a staircase for `stair_scum_alarm_turns` game turns, and so
     does "You hear a door burst open!" when no changed door places it;
  4. nothing left in the area: it takes a staircase (DL1: down when prepared
     for DL2, else up; deeper: up first), with no more detours on the way;
  5. on a staircase it rests there, and a threat the fight race did not take
     is left by the stairs (`leave_stairs_on_monster`);
  6. the mode ends at the threshold; C's leash is never removed.
  With no known route to a staircase, it explores toward the nearest known
  one and chases nothing.
- **The dial** (owner, 2026-11-23, after the review: "a tunable parameter to
  make it less strict"): `stair_scum_slack_steps`, the steps a grid may lie
  beyond the strict safe walk; 0 (shipped) is the reviewed strict count, 2
  the owner's "two or three steps" with a torch at 12 HP. With it a rate,
  `stair_scum_slack_per_hp_tenths` (owner: "a rate to make the scum
  dependent on hp/class"): tenths of a step per max HP point, per class and
  per race, so the mode loosens as the character grows. And by level,
  `stair_scum_slack_per_level_percent` (owner: "slack grows by decile by
  level, say 50 means that druids at level 3 get to slack 2"): percent of a
  step per character level above 1, rounded down. Default 50 (owner, 2026-11-23:
  "stair_scum_slack_per_level_percent: 50 as default"): it acts only with the
  mode on, so play is unchanged.
- **A race suffix for every parameter** (`params.rs`): `__<race>` / `@<race>`
  and `BORG_PARAM_<NAME>__<RACE>`, the `p_race.txt` names in lower case with
  `-` as `_`. Class value, then race value, then the common one;
  `params-core.json` gives `race_values` only where set; a unit test checks no
  race name is a class name. docs/ZOO-INTERFACE.md, "Race values".
- 9 parameters (registry 2461 with `track-p` `40c1c963`), each in `harness/borg.txt` at its default and
  in docs/CALIBRATION.md, "stair_scum (#1061)". `engine.rs` gains
  `race_ridx_if_set` (read only, beside `class_cidx_if_set`).

### Review (Codex, design review on #1061)

1. *[P1] budget the attacks on the move onto the staircase*:
   `attacks_on_retreat` counts them action by action from first sight
   through the stair command; Codex's case (seen 2 off, 12 HP, 8 per attack,
   3 steps) is lethal in the test, first sight at 1 and 2 both tested.
   `stair_scum_contact_damage` is now per attack round (default 4).
2. *[P1] the engine's light and the least visible approach*: the sight is
   the engine's radius whatever `cave_light_engine_radius` says, and in a lit
   room the distance to the nearest dark or unknown entry; tested with a dark
   side entrance.
3. *[P1] every voluntary step; no route*: the safe area and the step check in
   `borg_flow_old` (a resumed `GOAL_DARK` flow included); the walk excludes
   unknown grids and known traps; no route has its own outcome. Tests: a
   frontier behind an unknown grid, loot outside the area.
4. *[P2] the door sound has no location*: placed by a changed door seen since
   the sound, else the Borg goes onto the staircase; both cases tested.

### Review (Codex, implementation review on #1090 at `0726cba5`)

1. *[P1] a closed door in the retreat*: closed doors are off the retreat walk
   until open; a door target keeps its own budget (sight 1, the open).
2. *[P1] a staircase the Borg refuses*: one rule (`usable_kinds`) picks the
   staircases for the area, the walk back, the level change and the
   departure on a threat.
3. *[P1] detection learnt before it is used*: only the detection the
   exploration casts lifts the mode (the spell gate of
   `light_detect_evil_spell_min_level`, the Detection spell, the rod).
4. *[P2] chaining outward*: when the nearest target lies farther out than
   the Borg, it walks back onto the staircase first.

Each has a test (`a_closed_door_is_not_on_the_retreat`,
`the_area_counts_only_stairs_the_borg_takes`, `only_used_detection_lifts_the_mode`,
`between_targets_it_returns_toward_the_stairs`).

### Re-review (Codex, on #1090 at `30cece74`)

1. *[P1] the retreat can be cut off*: an occupied staircase is no escape;
   `cut_off` makes a grid unsafe when a monster from the frontier nearest its
   staircase gets there first (test `the_retreat_can_be_cut_off`).
2. *[P1] the return through a closed door*: the return steps over the retreat
   walk's own grids (`step_home`), and the step guard's "nearer" counts only
   those (test `the_return_walks_the_retreat_grids`).
3. *[P2] the return pre-empts a departure*: a committed `GOAL_BORE` departure
   resumes before the return rule.
4. *[P2] quiet door openings above CL5*: `track_closed` records doors while
   the mode applies, at any level.
5. *[P2] detection and the cast policy*: spells count only at a fail rate of
   40% or less (`borg_spell_legal_fail`), and the dark-entry cast no longer
   counts.

### Second re-review (Codex, on #1090 at `9276b75a`)

1. *[P1] a monster in a shortcut*: the retreat field is built over the grids
   the Borg can step on now (no monster), so the open detour is the walk and
   `step_home` follows it (test `a_monster_in_the_shortcut_leaves_the_detour`).
   The monsters' own way from the frontier keeps the terrain only.
2. *[P1] slack in the cut-off*: `retreat_ok` keeps the slack in the damage
   budget and times the cut-off on the real walk (test
   `the_cutoff_is_timed_on_the_real_walk`: a Mage's slack 2, a two-step walk,
   a monster one step from the staircase).

### Found

- **After the review, the mode is nearly pure stair scumming at CL1–3.** A
  torch lights 1 grid, a +10 monster may bite before the first step, so at
  12 HP and 4 per attack the safe walk is 1 step. Arriving on DL1 by its up
  staircase, the Borg often finds nothing safe and goes back up: the smoke
  Mage changes level 152 times in 20,000 turns (town and DL1), the three
  named seeds 39, 362 and 113 times in 100,000. XP comes from what wanders
  to the staircase, as the issue expected; how much it costs is the zoo's to
  measure. `stair_scum_slack_steps` is the dial (also
  `stair_scum_contact_damage` and `stair_scum_speed_ratio`).
- C's DL1 "grab an item before going back to town" (`borg_think_stair_scum`,
  before caution) walked 7 steps out; the filter now covers it.
- A vein by the staircase pulled the leaving walk back each turn (C's vein
  flow against the leave); veins are filtered, and once the mode leaves a
  level it takes no detours.
