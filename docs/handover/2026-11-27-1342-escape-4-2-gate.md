## 2026-11-27: Escape 4.2 findings, and the plan parked (#1342)

**Decision (owner, 2026-11-27).** Not to build the plan that this task wrote
up. It grew into a long list of guards (speed bounds, Slow and paralysis,
episode counters) for a gain that was small and unmeasured. The full plan, with
four Codex review rounds on it, is kept at commit `8a7b7072b021b3121558abc412c2206ffb187a73`
([the design page at that commit](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-reborn-rs/blob/8a7b7072b021b3121558abc412c2206ffb187a73/docs/design/ESCAPE-TIER-4-ACT-FIRST.md))
and in [PR 1345](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-reborn-rs/pull/1345). This
PR now only records the findings and the decision. No code and no setting
changed. It does not close #1342: the pack question there is still open.

**Found** (from the owner's evidence bundle for #1342, the Gnome Mage
`Borg261127142222`, build `9f55466f`, turns 1,368,600 to 1,467,919, and from
`borg-core/src/borg_escape.rs`):

- 24 Staff of Teleportation escapes: 15 at Danger Level 1, 5 at 2.1, 4 at 4.2.
  Tiers 1 and 2.1 fired at 1.25 to 30 times HP, real emergencies by the Borg's
  own estimate. The cost over the window: 24 charges, 5 Recharging casts (5 SP
  each, 23 charges gained). No death or serious damage followed any of them.
- Tier 4 ("compromised") fires on danger above 60% of current HP at clevel 35 or
  less, whatever the Borg's health. With `escape_phase_adjacent_rule` on, Phase
  Door is refused when no enemy is adjacent, and the escape then falls through
  to the long teleport (4.2) before the rule's own "attack at range or boost"
  (`phase_rule_act`, called from `borg_caution` only after `borg_escape`
  declines) can run. The same fall-through exists with an enemy adjacent and
  the fight not lost.
- The four 4.2 escapes: turn 1,368,677 (60/60 HP, danger 45, 0 without fear;
  nothing visible, after a retreat undone by the next flow step); 1,377,235
  (66/67, danger 66, 0 without fear; an unseen sound breath, 1 HP lost);
  1,403,464 (67/67, danger 54, 8 without fear; 16 cave spiders, the nearest 2
  grids away, speed 120 against 110); 1,443,183 (68/68, danger 48, 48 without
  fear; an orc tracker about 6 grids away, an attack available five thinks
  earlier, the monster picture 2,300 game turns old).
- Phase Door is not the answer for any of the four. Detect Monsters is cast
  often (73 times in the window) but was 2,300 to 3,600 game turns old before
  three of the four; in the excerpt the Borg held no Reveal Monsters, no
  detect-invisible item and no Potion of Speed until after all four.
- An earlier reading, that Identify Rune was holding three plain melee spares
  in the pack, does not fit the log: the Borg cast Identify Rune often and the
  Main Gauche and Rapiers show no `{??}`.
