## 2026-11-27: Plan to act before the compromised long teleport, escape 4.2 (#1342)

**Done.** [docs/design/ESCAPE-TIER-4-ACT-FIRST.md](../design/ESCAPE-TIER-4-ACT-FIRST.md)
describes how the compromised tier reaches the Staff of Teleportation, why,
and two settings groups (all off by default) that would make the Borg look,
shoot or boost first. No code and no shipped setting changed. It does not
close #1342: the pack question in that issue (slots `v` and `w`) is still
open. An earlier draft of the page proposed refusing 4.2 on the damage-only
danger; the owner's reading is that all four cases are real threats, so that
gate is left out (the page says why).

**Found** (read from the owner's evidence bundle for #1342 and from
`borg-core/src/borg_escape.rs` at `9f55466f`; the details are in the plan):

- The 24 staff escapes were 15 at tier 1, 5 at 2.1 and 4 at 4.2. Tiers 1
  and 2.1 look like real emergencies by the Borg's estimate.
- With `escape_phase_adjacent_rule` on, Phase Door is refused at range and
  the escape falls through to the long teleport before the rule's own
  "attack at range or boost" (`phase_rule_act`) can run. Every other answer
  waits until no escape acted.
- Case B (turn 1,377,235) was an unseen sound breath, not necessarily an
  invisible monster. In the excerpt the Borg held no Reveal Monsters, no
  detect-invisible item and no Potion of Speed at A to C. Detect Monsters
  (2 SP) is cast often (73 times in the window), but was 2,300 to 3,600 game
  turns old before B, C and D.
- Case C's spiders were 2 to 3 grids away, not 5 or more (an error in the
  first draft of the plan, found in review). The danger sum's reach test
  simulates one step, so it cannot certify a wait against fast monsters; the
  plan adds a reach rule, bounded by the engine's energy schedule and counted in
  Chebyshev grids (re-review).
- An earlier reading in this investigation, that Identify Rune was holding
  three plain melee spares in the pack, does not fit the log: the Borg cast
  Identify Rune often and the Main Gauche and Rapiers show no `{??}`.
