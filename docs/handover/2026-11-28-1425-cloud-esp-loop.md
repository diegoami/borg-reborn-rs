## 2026-11-28: A wasted cast is not a miss (#1425)

All checks ran on the default core, **borg-rs** (`borg-core/`, `harness/pborg.yaml`).

### What was found

Hobbit Druid `Borg261128174030` (P build `4101f6ec`, borg-cemetery#168)
stood at (159,31) on DL7 and cast Stinking Cloud at a gallant at (175,23)
3,490 times. It sensed the gallant by ESP.

- **The engine.** A temporary probe in `main-borgh.c` loaded the case's save
  and printed the engine's view of the shot. The probe was never committed,
  and the build was redone without it. `los` = 0, `projectable` = 0, and the
  gallant was visible but not in view. `project_path` runs (160,31),
  (161,30), then **granite at (162,30)**, which the player has never seen.
  The radius-2 ball bursts at (161,30), 14 grids short of the gallant.
- **The Borg.** After every cast, `borg_update` found no hit message. With
  `update_miss_wall_guess = 1` (#929, shipped by #1069), a monster in
  `BORG_VIEW` that was seen at the shot counts as a miss, not a wall:
  `successful_target` becomes 2 and no wall is guessed. 55 of the 59 casts
  kept in the excerpts end with that note; the other 4 were spell failures.
  `BORG_VIEW` passes through unknown grids (`sight_grid`), and ESP shows the
  gallant every think, so the next cast was always allowed. #929's reason
  holds only for missiles: a fired or thrown miss prints nothing. A spell or
  device has no hit roll (`project_path`, project.c:205-218; a ball bursts
  before the wall, :693-696).

**The recurrence at turn 3,511,372** (same package, second archive). The
same Druid at (54,27), in a north–south corridor, cast 2,772 times at a
soldier at (55,46), also sensed by ESP. Of the 27 recorded casts, 24 end in
the same note. The same probe on that save gives `los` = 0, and the soldier
is visible but not in view. The path runs over known floor (54,28)–(54,34),
then unseen floor at (54,35) and (54,36), then **granite at (55,37)**,
unknown to the player.

### What was done

`borg_update.rs`: the "miss, not a wall" note is given only when the shot
was a missile (`successful_target` -2 before the -10). A cast that did no
damage now takes C's wall guess, still under #929's guard. Its first unknown
grid is (162,30), the engine's blocker. Granite there stops the next ball
path (`borg_launch_bolt_at_location` scores it 0). The map pass never writes
an unknown screen grid back, so the Borg stops casting from that grid. Tests:
`a_wasted_cast_is_not_a_miss`, `a_wasted_cloud_guesses_the_engines_blocker`,
`the_borg_walks_the_engines_path_to_the_gallant` (the Borg's path walk
equals the engine's three steps), and
`the_corridor_cloud_guesses_the_engines_blocker` (the recurrence: the ten
engine steps match, and C's hallway rule skips the two unseen floor grids in
the Borg's own column, so the guess is (55,37), the real wall). In general
the guess is the first unknown grid off the hallway, which may be floor.
Granite there still stops the casts from that grid, and the map pass replaces
it once the Borg sees the grid. The `params.rs` description,
`docs/CALIBRATION.md` and `docs/c-borg/borg-rs-c-bugs.md` §41 now state the
narrower rule.

Smoke on the default core: 4/4 runs identical to `smoke-borg-rs`, including
messages.log, so no re-record is needed. CI's fmt, clippy and both test
commands pass.

### What was not checked

- No native replay of the loop. The save restores the engine but not the
  Borg's memory, and the fresh-memory continuation never targets the gallant
  (borg-cemetery#168). The fix is checked at the decision level, on the
  case's geometry and the engine's path.
- No zoo measurement. The change applies only where a spell or device does
  no damage at a monster in view.

### What is left, and owner decisions

- #1430: a missile at a monster sensed only by ESP behind unknown ground is
  still taken for a miss. A stricter test (every grid of the path known and
  open) changed 2 of 4 smoke games: an ordinary flask miss across dark floor
  got a granite guess. It was dropped. The options in #1430 include reading
  the engine's "directly seen" monster flag, which widens perception (T2.5).
  That is the owner's call.

### Review by the owner (at `12fda44f`, relayed on the PR), and the fixes

1. **Scare Monster.** It deals no damage, and the Borg parsed "flees in
   terror!" without clearing the pending shot, so the new fallback could
   guess a wall on a legal path. A fear message from the goal's monster now
   counts as reached.
2. **Leap into Battle.** It sets -1, moves the player and swings
   (`effect_handler_MOVE_ATTACK`); its "You miss" left the shot pending. A
   melee `HIT` or `MISS` at the goal's monster now counts as reached. Both
   go through `goal_reached` (test `fear_and_a_melee_swing_show_the_shot_reached`).
3. **A target straight down the Borg's corridor** (found on #1432, fixed
   here, because a cast takes the same walk). The hallway rule passed over
   every unknown grid, and C's walk then guessed the step back from the
   target. When the player remembers that grid as floor, the map pass of the
   same update writes it back (`screen_overwrites`, pulled out of
   `update_map_grid` unchanged), and the shot stays eligible. With the
   guard, the walk now guesses the first unknown grid it passed over. Test
   `a_corridor_guess_survives_the_map_pass_and_stops_the_next_aim` follows
   the guess through a modelled map pass to the next aim's path walk. The
   real aim and map pass need the engine; they are modelled, not run.
