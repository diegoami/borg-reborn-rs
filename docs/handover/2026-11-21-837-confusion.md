## 2026-11-21: Confusion switches, all shipped 0 (#837)

**Done.** Six new settings, each compiled and shipped 0 (today's play), from
the owner's wishes in #837. Their registry rows and descriptions are in
`docs/CALIBRATION.md` ("Confusion (#837)"); the five `caution_*` ones are in
`borg-core/src/borg_caution.rs`, the town one in `borg_town_capability.rs`.

| setting | what |
|---|---|
| `caution_confused_clw_cure` | a Cure Light Wounds is a confusion cure (20 turns off), in both chains of `borg_heal` and the cures at the end of `borg_caution`, after Clear Mind, CSW and CCW |
| `caution_confused_cure_before_walk` | a confused Borg carrying a cure does not start a "Desperate for Stairs" walk before it |
| `caution_confusion_cure_when_stuck` | next to an immobile confuser the cure is held (#646) only while a step out of reach can still be taken (gap 1: the deadlock) |
| `caution_immobile_step_mobile_first` | the step away ranks grids by the danger from monsters that move first, then the stairs (gap 2) |
| `town_confusion_cure_reserve`, `_percent` | the first N confusion cures in the pack are a requirement (as Recall's reserve), so the first trip buys one and the Home does not take it |

`immobile_step_away`'s grid choice is now `best_step_away`, which records
nothing; `confusion_cure_held` asks it too. With every switch at 0 it picks
the same grid as before.

**Not done, on purpose.**
- "Don't melee while confused except as a last resort" is not a switch: with
  a cure in reach it is the cure's job (the first setting), and without one
  every action goes astray the same way.
- The kobold shaman is two scary-list lines, not code and not shipped
  (`attack_on_sight` ships 1, so a line in `harness/scary_monsters.txt` would
  change shipped play): an attack entry or a scare, for a zoo arm to append
  (borg-zoo#228). A unit test parses both over the shipped list.
- Proposed item 4 (not confused, next to the confuser: one deterministic step
  out of reach) is #838's category.
