## 2026-11-23: A trap that cuts the way back (#1067)

Track P, P8. Every check below ran on the **default core, borg-rs**; none on
the reborn core. All three rules are compiled and shipped **0**: shipped play
is unchanged (smoke 4/4 identical to `smoke-borg-rs`; the death seed plays
the shipped game key for key with the switches off).

**Reproduced.** Half-Elf Warrior seed 1868115537 (`-race 1 -class 0`, menu,
exact, 100,000 turns) on track-p `f67fad19` plays the keys of the Cemetery's
head replay (`h-base`, `55c065a9dc5e9c2d`): the poison pit at turn 11,099,
24 "Bored but unable to flow to up stairs.", dead at 11,236.

**What was done.** New module `borg-core/src/borg_trap_way.rs`, one hook in
the dungeon think (after the flight, the caution and the light, before the
recovery flows), one in `borg_parse_aux` (trap messages), and a flow flag
(`through_traps`) that lets only this module's spread cross known traps
(`spread_blocked`). Seven parameters (docs/CALIBRATION.md, "Traps that cut
the way back"):

1. `trap_recover` (+ `trap_recover_max_hp`, 60): below 60 max HP, a trap that
   hurts sends the Borg back to the nearest staircase to cure or rest; it
   takes the staircase at the first threat and explores again only whole.
2. `trap_way_back` (+ `trap_disarm_min_percent` 50, `trap_disarm_max_tries`
   10): when every route to every known staircase crosses a known trap the
   flows refuse, the first trap of the route is cleared first: `D` at the
   engine's chance, `W` onto a trap whose kind cannot kill at the current HP,
   or rest and disarm whole.
3. `trap_leave_level_count` (+ `trap_leave_near_count` 0 and
   `trap_leave_radius` 10, added in review): the n-th distinct trap of the
   level, or the m-th near the arrival grid, makes a weak Borg leave by the
   nearest staircase it may take once it can reach one.

### Review round (Codex GPT-6, three comments on #1086, all at `ec6f8957`)

Merged track-p `09580159` (conflicts in `params.rs` and `docs/CALIBRATION.md`:
citations, taken from track-p, then `check-param-citations.py --fix`; both
CALIBRATION sections kept). Every finding was accepted:

- **Resting while poison or a cut ticks** (P1; the reviewer withdrew the
  "rests through every tick" half: a damaging tick breaks `R&`). `rest_here`
  now gives `borg_recover` (cures, its guards) the turn first.
- **Stairs the Borg cannot take** (P2). One set of usable staircases (up
  unless descent is forced; down only when not rising and not
  `more_refused`) drives the cut-off probe, the walk back (own spread,
  `GOAL_RECOVER`; no more `borg_flow_stair_both`) and the exit.
- **Item flows first** (P2). The hook moved before the item flows (after
  the attack and wear checks). Since it now runs before `borg_check_light`,
  rule 2 steps aside without light instead of resting.
- **A trap that survives `W`** (P2). With `trap_way_back` on, `spread_blocked`
  lets a flow leave the trap under the Borg (`trap_underfoot_open`) and still
  refuses every other; test `a_trap_that_survives_the_step_is_left_not_cleared`.
- **Disarm attribution** (P2). `borg_play_step`'s own `D` now names its
  target (`note_disarm`), every target is consumed at the next think, and a
  disarm message with no target is not placed (`trigger_grid`).
- **The radius did nothing** (P3). `trap_leave_near_count` is a separate
  threshold; the test now uses reachable states.
- **Teleport rune at the landing grid** (P2, re-review). A walk activation is
  placed at the step's grid (`goal.g`, one grid from where the Borg stood
  when the message arrived), so one rune set off twice is one trap; test
  `a_teleport_rune_is_its_own_grid_not_the_landing`.

Not covered by a test: an integration case with a refused down staircase and
a trap before the only up staircase (no engine-free cave fixture: the spread
reads the danger through the engine). The unit tests cover the pure parts.
