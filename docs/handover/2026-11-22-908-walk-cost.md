# 2026-11-22 — #908: price the "Desperate for Stairs (three)" walk against a faster pursuer

**Issue:** diegoami/borg-reborn-rs#908. **Branch:** `task/T908-walk-cost`,
stacked on #907 (`task/T907-haste-speed`). Track P, P7.

## What was done

New parameter `caution_desperate_walk_cost_percent`, compiled and shipped 0.

- **How it decides.** In branch (three) of `desperate_for_stairs`,
  `desperate_walk_refused` adds up the expected HP per step from every awake,
  mobile monster next to the Borg. Each one costs its damage per action
  (`fight_incoming`, haste counted) × `max(0, actions per Borg step − 1)`.
  Above 0, the walk is refused when steps × that sum reaches the percent of
  the HP.
- **The note.** "Desperate walk refused: … (#908)".
- **What follows.** The refusal falls through to the rest of `borg_caution`:
  retreat, `borg_escape` (Phase Door), the Phase rule, back-away and #457's
  outmatched heal.
- **When.** It is asked every think.
- **Tests.** Unit tests cover the step cost, using the case's numbers.

## What was found

Rogue 381988580 (borg-cemetery `runs/2026-11-22-fastfrag-rogue-381988580`):
13 steps × 4.5 HP against HP 23. In the counterfactual branches, a Phase
Door read instead of the walk at 62,909 or 62,919 survives. A Phase Door at
62,899 followed by the same walk dies.

## Checked on the exact game

The five branches' code was ported onto 7f8c6b80 (the death's build) in a
scratch tree, with the switches set through `BORG_PARAM_*`. With everything
off it replays the death's keys exactly.

- **This switch alone (100).** The walk is refused from 62,899 ("13 steps x
  4.50 HP/step = 58.5 >= 100% of HP 23"). But `retreat` then steps away
  instead for three turns, because `borg_danger` prices the next grid at the
  soldier's base speed ("41 > 0"). A Phase Door comes at HP 7, and the Rogue
  lives to the 100,000-turn cap.
- **With #907's `danger_observed_haste` as well.** Danger 84 at 62,899: no
  retreat, a Phase Door at once at HP 23, and it lives to the cap.

So this switch is best measured together with `danger_observed_haste`.

## What is left

- **Not measured** in the zoo (RULES 14, 20), and the 381988580 encounter is
  not replayed with the switch on (a borg.txt change alters the game from
  birth).
- **Not priced:** monsters that are not adjacent yet, ranged attacks, and a
  path longer than the straight-line distance (the steps are a lower bound,
  which errs toward walking).
- **Other walks.** Branches (one) and (two) (stairs within 2–3 steps) are
  unchanged.

## Owner decisions

The value to measure (100 is the case's break-even; a hypothesis), and
whether to ship it.
