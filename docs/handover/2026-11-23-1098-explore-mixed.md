## 2026-11-23: mixed exploration from the staircases (#1098, from #1080)

**Done.** The second mode of #1080, in `borg_flow_dark.rs`, behind
`explore_mixed` (compiled 0, and `harness/borg.txt` ships 0). Its six numbers
are `explore_mix_radius` (5), `explore_mix_step` (2), `explore_mix_sight`
(5), `explore_mix_speed_ratio` (2), `explore_mix_contact_damage` (5) and
`explore_mix_max_radius` (30). The names are #1098's, except
`explore_mix_speed_ratio`, which is its *r*. The rules are in
`docs/CALIBRATION.md` ("Mixed exploration from the staircases").
- **Where it applies.** The switch is on, breadth-first does not apply (max HP
  at or above the class's threshold, or its switch off), and the Borg is not
  yet prepared for the depth.
- **The walk.** Depth-first (the interesting grid nearest the Borg) only among
  grids within a radius *R* of the nearest known staircase of either kind.
  - A branch whose frontier lies past *R* is parked, and the Borg walks back
    to another branch.
  - *R* starts at `explore_mix_radius` and grows by `explore_mix_step` only
    when nothing reachable is left inside it.
  - *R* stops at the escape model's limit, ⌊*D* / (*r* − 1)⌋ + ⌊max HP /
    *c*⌋, capped by `explore_mix_max_radius` and by C's leash.
  - With nothing left within the limit, the level is left.
- **Shared with breadth-first.** Its frame (rings, candidates, reach, return
  leash: `stair_frame`) and its tries (`run_tries`) are now shared with
  breadth-first. Breadth-first plays the same keys as before.

**Choices the owner may want to change.**
1. **"Prepared for the depth"**: #1080's readiness model does not exist.
   The stand-in is "where C's exploring leash applies" (`c_leash_on`: depth
   ≥ CL − `flow_leash_depth_margin`, CL < `flow_leash_stairs_max_level`, or
   the kit short). Elsewhere exploration is C's depth-first order, as today.
2. **How I read *R***: #1098 says "*R* starts at `explore_mix_radius` and
   comes from the escape model". Here the start is `explore_mix_radius`, and
   the escape model is the **limit** *R* grows to (never below the start).
   The other reading, the model as the start and `explore_mix_max_radius` as
   the only bound, would let every Borg reach 30 steps once the near area is
   done.
3. **Max HP, not current HP**, in *k*: the radius does not shrink while the
   Borg is wounded (it rests first). With current HP, a wounded Borg would
   walk back toward the stairs.
4. **The radius is not stored**: each think takes the smallest radius that
   holds a candidate. A branch newly found near the stairs (a door, a side
   passage seen from the corridor) comes first again.
5. **Depth band**: *c* is `explore_mix_contact_damage` times ⌈DL / 5⌉, and
   *r* is one value at every depth (2 is right at DL1–5: speed 120 is 20
   energy against 10, `game-world.c:68`). Deeper, a band table may be
   wanted.
