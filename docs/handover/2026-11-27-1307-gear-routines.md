# Stop gear cleanup routines from blocking or undoing each other

Issue #1307, the first of four fixes from the equipment routine audit of
`8d85f44f`. #1308, #1309 and #1310 follow as stacked PRs.

## Done

- `planned_pack_excess` no longer skips a slot whose plan wears a pack
  upgrade. `borg_wear_stuff` returns before its ranked branch when hungry
  or weak, after `wear_best_max_turns` on a level or after
  `wear_best_max_bounces`. Until now no lower-ranked spare in that slot left
  for the rest of the level. The plan keeps the worn piece and the upgrade,
  so the gear it releases ranks below both.
- The ranked `gear_plan` release and `redundant_copies` keep the active
  weapon and armour swap, as the other cleanup paths already did.
- In the unresolved melee path, a Home alternative counts as cover whatever
  the carry allowance, and a stacked pack alternative whatever the free pack
  slots. A Home offer the shop rejected still covers nothing. The wear and
  withdrawal checks are unchanged.
- `surplus_light` no longer releases permanent lights. The ranked plan
  owns them, so one permanent backup stays. Earlier the light reserve
  dropped a same-kind permanent light that the ranked plan had chosen as
  the backup.
- With `birth_randarts`, `redundant_copies` no longer counts a partly known
  artifact weapon as a better peer. Wear already excluded it.

## Checked

The native `ranked-cleanup` case in `engine/tests/test_exploration_pack.py`
runs the shipped ranked policy. It checks a timed-out level, the armour
swap in both ranked paths, cover with a heavy pack and with a full pack,
and two permanent lights. The randarts condition has no native test,
because the fixture cannot change a birth option.

## Left

- #1308, #1309 and #1310 continue the audit's fixes.
- The fuelled light reserve still counts a carried permanent light as cover
  without checking that the ranked plan keeps it. It matters only when two
  permanent lights are carried, so no issue was opened.
