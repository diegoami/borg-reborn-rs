## 2026-11-23: poison triage (#1140)

Owner, 2026-11-23: "even without a poison cure they could have kept
themselves alive with normal health potions"; "They just die of Poison even
if not attacked."

**Done.** New module `borg-core/src/borg_poison.rs`, five switches, all
compiled and shipped 0. At the shipped settings play is unchanged: smoke 4/4
identical to `smoke-borg-rs`, and the three named deaths replay key for key
with every switch 0 (below).
- **A, the pending damage** (`poison_pending_note`).
  - The HP the poison still takes is `ceil(counter / (adj_con_fix[CON] + 1))`.
    The counter is the engine's `player_timed(TMD_POISONED)`, the owner's
    choice for #1136.
  - The healing carried counts each CLW (20 HP) and CSW (40 HP) at most at
    `max HP - 1`.
  - A cure carried or castable (`BI_ACUREPOIS`, `BI_ACCW`, Healing, *Healing*,
    Life, Cure Poison, Herbal Curing) or Minor Healing turns every rule off.
  - The note is written when any switch is on, once per change.
  - `adj_con_fix` and the two heals are pinned to the engine source by unit
    tests.
- **B, drink late** (`poison_late_drink`, `poison_late_drink_margin` = 2).
  - Applies when the Borg is poisoned, not cut, carries no cure and stands at
    danger 0.
  - `borg_heal`'s CLW/CSW tiers then hold the potion, unless the whole heal
    fits under the max HP.
  - At HP ≤ the next turn's poison + margin, `late_drink` drinks one directly.
    The tiers refuse a CLW at a large loss past CL10.
- **C, leave while it can be fixed** (`poison_leave_unfixable`).
  - Trigger: the pending poison is at least HP + the healing carried.
  - Then: leaving, fleeing and `rising`, without waiting for half HP and a 25%
    roll.
  - Recall is read below DL1, or on DL1 with no known up staircase.
- **D, town triage** (`poison_town_triage`). The trigger is being in town
  with the pending poison at least the HP.
  - `borg_town_live::choose` first buys at the Alchemist, before the excess
    trip and the plan. It reads the Borg's record of the stock and buys a
    Neutralize Poison, else a CCW if affordable, else the CLW that make
    HP + healing exceed the poison. The executor caps the copies at the gold.
  - At most 4 triage purchases per visit.
  - A bought cure is drunk outside the store (`town_cure`, before
    `borg_recover`, whose costly cure needs a 25% roll and 2 CCW).
  - A store is left when a drink is due.
  - CLW are drunk late, as in B, even with B off.
  - Under `birth_no_selling` nothing is sold (`kit_of`'s sale shop is none)
    and no excess trip is taken.
- **E, the poison fight race** (`poison_fight_pending`). It applies when the
  Borg is poisoned, carries no cure and stands next to awake, visible
  poisoners.
  - Lost when turns to kill × (damage + new poison over the CON decay) +
    the pending poison reach HP + healing.
  - New poison per hit is `5 + 1d(rlev)` (mon-blows.c:683-684), weighted by
    the hit chance and the monster's actions per turn.
  - A lost race starts leaving and fleeing. Next to a poisoner that never
    moves, the Borg steps out of reach (`best_step_away_from`, as #1125) before
    any cure.
  - An unknown health or attack leaves the fight to the other rules.

**Decided here, for the owner to confirm.**
- **"Safe" for B is danger 0.** A cat in town is enough to keep today's
  tiers drinking. A looser test (danger below the HP the potion would waste)
  is a possible follow-up.
- **When C reads Recall.** Below DL1 always. On DL1 only with no known up
  staircase; otherwise it walks. The Recall delay (15-35 turns) is poison
  paid either way.
- **D's limits.**
  - D trusts the Borg's record of the Alchemist's stock. A stock it has never
    seen is not visited for the triage.
  - D counts the cure purchase only in the store, at most 4 per visit.
- **The no-sale rule applies only while the triage holds** (pending ≥ HP),
  not always under `birth_no_selling`. A sale for 0 gold still identifies a
  flavour.
- **E is in borg_caution**, after the early fights (`fight_before_flight`)
  and before the heal. It counts every adjacent poisoner's turns to kill one
  after another, as #759 does.
