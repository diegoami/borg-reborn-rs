**Done.** New module `borg-core/src/borg_poison.rs`, behind **one switch**,
`deal_with_poison`, compiled and shipped 0. Two values tune it:
`poison_late_drink_margin` (2 HP) and `poison_cure_pending_percent` (50).
At the shipped settings play is unchanged:
- smoke is 4/4 identical to `smoke-borg-rs`;
- the four named deaths and the 18 survivors replay key for key with the
  switch at 0 (below).

With the switch on:
- **A, the pending damage.**
  - The HP the poison still takes is `ceil(counter / (adj_con_fix[CON] + 1))`.
    The counter is the engine's `player_timed(TMD_POISONED)`, the owner's
    choice for #1136.
  - The healing carried counts each CLW (20 HP) and CSW (40 HP) at most at
    `max HP - 1`.
  - A note is written while poisoned.
  - `adj_con_fix` and the two heals are pinned to the engine source by unit
    tests.
- **F, use a cure it carries.** Once the poison still to come is 50 % of
  the HP, the Borg uses a poison cure at once, whatever the danger and with
  no roll. Order:
  - the spells, a rod of Curing, an activation, a food or mushroom with
    `CURE:POISONED`;
  - Neutralize Poison, a staff of Curing;
  - CCW (the raw quaff: `borg_quaff_crit` keeps the last two) or its
    activation;
  - Healing, *Healing* or Life, only when the CLW/CSW cannot outlast the
    poison.

  Never next to an awake poisoner, which would poison it again at once.
  With a cure carried, B, C and E stand aside.
- **B, drink late.**
  - Applies when the Borg is poisoned, not cut, carries no cure and stands at
    danger 0.
  - The CLW/CSW tiers then hold the potion unless its whole heal fits under
    the max HP.
  - At HP ≤ the next turn's poison + margin, `late_drink` drinks one.
- **C, leave while it can be fixed.**
  - Trigger: the pending poison is at least HP + healing.
  - Then: leaving, fleeing and `rising`.
  - Recall is read below DL1, or on DL1 with no known up staircase.
- **D, town triage.** The trigger is being in town with the pending poison
  at least the HP.
  - The Alchemist comes first (`borg_town_live::choose`). The Borg buys a
    Neutralize Poison, else a CCW if affordable, else enough CLW, at most
    4 purchases per visit.
  - A bought cure is drunk outside the store, and a store is left when a
    drink is due.
  - Under `birth_no_selling` nothing is sold and no excess trip is taken.
- **E, the poison fight race.** It applies when the Borg is next to awake
  poisoners.
  - Lost when turns to kill × (damage + new poison over the CON decay) +
    the pending poison reach HP + healing.
  - A lost race starts leaving. Next to a poisoner that never moves, the
    Borg steps out of reach before any cure.
- Docs: `params.rs`, `docs/CALIBRATION.md` (a section, with the C Borg's
  handling), `harness/borg.txt`, `scripts/borg-core-vendor.py`. The
  parameter test maps the `deal` prefix to `borg_poison.rs`.

**Review of #1146** (owner's reviewer, at `c7606c20`; five findings, all
fixed in `b59d483c`):
1. The healing budget credited `max HP - 1` a potion; B drinks at the line
   (3 HP), so a CLW at max HP 19 restores 16, not 18. Now `max HP - line`
   (`late_heal`), in the unfixable test, C, D's CLW count and the note.

   made the parts stand aside. Now a cure counts only when usable now

   the potion or food carried); healing spells are not counted.

   waits while poisoned next to an awake poisoner, with a cure due, or past
   HP + healing (`defers_early_fight`).

   longest delay (34 world ticks; 15 with no up staircase known;
   `recall_survivable`), else the stairs.
5. A rod or staff of Curing alone did not count as a cure. Now it does
   (`borg_equips_rod`, `borg_equips_staff_fail`).
