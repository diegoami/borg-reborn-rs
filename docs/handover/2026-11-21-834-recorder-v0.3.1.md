## 2026-11-21: adopt recorder v0.3.1 (#834)

**Done:** a pin update and nothing else. `vendor/angband-recorder` and
`recorder-pin.json` are v0.3.1 (`88c9324b`, 58 files, sealed byte for byte by
the external checker), the build label is `borg-reborn-recorder-v0.3.1`, and
docs/ANGBAND-RECORDER.md and docs/PROVENANCE.md say so.

**Why:** a recording of an X11 game window that was closed or killed was
sealed but ended at the last command/wait frame, so what happened after it
(a death included) was missing. v0.3.0 only fixed that for the headless
frontend. v0.3.1 writes the closing `final` frame from the exit handler too
(angband-recorder#15, release notes of v0.3.1). No interface change.

**Checked here:** both pin checkers; the recorder-enabled build; and
`harness/check-recorder.py` (headless frontend only). In the recorder
repository, on a scratch build with this recorder code under a virtual
display: a killed window ends with `final`, a Ctrl-X quit with `exit`.

**Not done:**
- The local launcher `angband-reborn-record` still runs its previous install;
  to get this, build a revision with this pin and run `install-launcher.sh`
  (recorder repository, `docs/borg-reborn-rs-recording/`).
- Not checked: replay of a windowed recording that ended by a closed window,
  a window on a real display, a death in a window, a kill by signal.
- #356's guided-session `ANGBAND_SAVE_*` settings are still open.

**Known (recorder side):** `angband-recorder verify` of a replay made in
another directory reports `combat.jsonl` and `world` differences
(angband-recorder#11); the recorder's X11 test sometimes timed out in its CI
(angband-recorder#13).
