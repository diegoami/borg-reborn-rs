## 2026-11-24: Price a desperate up-stair walk (#908)

The standalone `caution_desperate_walk_cost_percent` experiment
compiles and ships 0. Branch (three) can refuse a planned up-stair route
when its steps, priced against awake adjacent mobile monsters' excess
melee actions at their current observed speeds, reach the configured
share of HP. The immediate known-up-stair handoff respects that refusal.
An eligible down stair, retreat, escape and cures are still available
in the existing order. At 100, the 13-step Rogue case prices 58.5 HP
against 23 HP.

The price uses expected HP from melee blows and assumes the pursuer spends
one action per step following. The budget assigns no additional cost to
ranged spells or unpriced status effects. The stair fixture places a
closer unreachable up stair beside a reachable 13-step corridor and
exercises the handoff at weights 0 and 5, with the budget off and with a
pursuer slowed by the engine's maximum Slow level. The older walk-only
arm first fell through to strategic retreat. The arm also counting
observed haste in danger used Phase Door at once. Compare these two
handoffs on a pinned current scenario before making a policy decision.
