## 2026-11-24: Price a desperate up-stair walk (#908)

The standalone `caution_desperate_walk_cost_percent` experiment
compiles and ships 0. Branch (three) can refuse to walk when a
straight-line lower bound on the moves to a free up stair, priced
against awake adjacent mobile monsters' excess melee actions, reaches
the configured share of HP. The original order of stair options,
retreat, escape and cures applies after a refusal. At 100, the
13-step Rogue case prices 58.5 HP against 23 HP. The focused regression
covers this estimate, a shorter walk, a slower pursuer and 0.

The price ignores detours and uses the existing mean melee estimate
with a corridor-following assumption. This is a trial bound and may
miss a costly longer route. The older exact case's walk-only arm first
fell through to strategic retreat. The arm also counting observed
haste in danger used Phase Door at once. Compare these two handoffs on
a pinned current scenario before making a policy decision.
