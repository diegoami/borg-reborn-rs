## #842: fast-fragile monsters, by name and by stats (borg-zoo#566's 8 crow deaths)

Branch `task/842-fast-fragile` (pushed as `claude/youthful-brahmagupta-j7a2y1`),
PR linked from the issue. Owner, 2026-11-21: attack a fast, fragile monster
"with all that we have got, unless we want a phase door or a stair", and never
drink a cure against it. Everything is behind switches shipped **0**: with them
off, the 8 cases are the zoo's games key for key, and the smoke suite at the
shipped settings is 3/3 identical to `smoke-borg-rs` (no re-record).

### What was built

New module `borg-core/src/borg_fast_fragile.rs` (ours, registered in
`scripts/borg-core-vendor.py`), small hooks in `borg_fight_attack.rs`
(`attack_on_sight`), `borg_caution.rs` (`category_attack_safe_with`, the heal
sites) and `scary_list.rs` (`attack_named`). Parameters (CALIBRATION.md,
"Fast-fragile monsters (#842)"):

| parameter | default | what |
|---|---:|---|
| `fast_fragile_policy` | 0 | 1: a marked monster is attacked with the focused planner's best attack every turn, without `target damage < HP` (the other foes' danger test stays; HP 0 is alive); the only alternative is the staircase underfoot, else Phase Door, when the next turn kills and the kill needs more than one more turn |
| `fast_fragile_by_stats` | 0 | 1: mark by stats besides the named `fast-fragile` entries (crow, wild cat) |
| `fast_fragile_no_heal` | 0 | 1: no cure (`borg_heal`, the outmatched heal) while a marked monster is awake next to the Borg; 2: the same, except when cornered (next turn kills, kill needs more than one turn, no Phase Door or stairs): a cure first |
| `fast_fragile_escape_percent` | 100 | "next turn kills" = expected damage of the marked monsters within 2 grids reaches this percent of HP; 0 = no Phase Door / stairs alternative |
| `fast_fragile_speed_margin` | 5 | speed points over the Borg's |
| `fast_fragile_kill_turns` | 2 | HP at most this many turns of the best attack (hit chance, failure, HP cap) |
| `fast_fragile_damage_percent` | 25 | expected damage per Borg turn at least this percent of the current HP |
| `fast_fragile_group_max` | 2 | at most this many others of its race in view (swarm cut) |

The characterization also needs: mobile, not unique, not a breeder
(`MULTIPLY`). A scary-list attack entry of any reason **names** its monster and
overrides the stats (a name can add or remove a monster). Every mark is noted
with its numbers: `# Fast-fragile (named): crow (speed +9 vs +0, 11 HP vs 8.9
per turn, 7.2 damage/turn = 66% of HP 11, 0 others in view) (#842)`.
Unit tests cover the verdict (crow at CL1 yes; CL20, a swarm, +1 speed, no attack
no) and the escape rule.

### What was found

**Local replays** of the 8 crow cases on `ca89e738`, 100,000 turns, switches
only (canonical report: [borg-cemetery PR #102](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-cemetery/pull/102), `studies/fast-fragile-842`, the table and the commands;
direction only, 8 chosen cases, not a measurement):

| arm | dead of 8 | to crow/cat |
|---|---:|---:|
| shipped | 8 | 7 |
| policy, no Phase/stairs alternative (`escape_percent=0`) | 6 | 5 |
| **policy, named list** | **1** | 0 |
| policy + `no_heal=1` | 1 | 0 |
| policy + `no_heal=2` (cornered exception) | 3 | 2 |
| policy, `escape_percent=75` | **0** | 0 |
| policy + stats | 1 | 0 |
| policy + stats + `no_heal=1` | 1 | 0 |
| `no_heal=1` alone | 5 | 4 |

1. **The ungated attack alone is not enough; Phase Door is what saves the
   games.** Without the alternative 6 of 8 still die (the Borg attacks at HP 3).
   With it the Druid 1311931798 reads Phase 4 times (it had 8) and lives.
2. **The heal ban never binds once the policy is on:** the attack comes before
   `borg_caution`, so the output is identical to the policy alone. It binds only
   when the attack does not happen (no attack, other danger): alone, it
   leaves 5 of 8 dead. Not a reason to ship it separately, but it is the
   owner's rule and costs nothing with the policy on.
3. **The cornered exception (`no_heal=2`) is worse:** Ranger 526144893 and
   Kobold Priest 994753489 die again, by the cure loop (cure, four bites, cure).
   Attacking is better than curing when cornered. Not recommended.
4. **"Next turn kills" at the mean is too late for four bites.** The Dunadan
   Priest 1309976551 dies identically in every arm at 100: 7.2 damage a turn
   against 8 HP, one oil thrown (missed), the Phase Door it carried unread. At
   `escape_percent=75` it reads Phase and all 8 live. One case; a hypothesis for
   the zoo to measure.
5. **Why melee beats carried oil (Necromancer 1705507033, 34 flasks): not a bug.**
   `focused_attack` scores `min(damage, HP) × hit chance`. The character's throwing
   skill is Necromancer 20 + Half-Troll −10 = 10 (`class.txt:966`,
   `p_race.txt:227`), against melee 35 + 20 = 55: vs the crow's AC 14 oil hits
   20.3% (1.83 HP/turn, 9 HP crow) and the Main Gauche 4.09. The ranking is right
   on the game's numbers; "oil before melee" would halve this character's damage.
   What killed it was the heal loop and the gate, which the policy removes (with it
   the Necromancer survives to CL6). Not changed.
6. **The stats rule marked, besides crows and wild cats, cave spiders** (6 notes:
   `cave spider (speed +9 vs +0, 2 HP vs 1.5 per turn, 5.2 damage/turn = 35% of HP
   15, 0 others in view)`, when the swarm had thinned to one). A first version
   also marked a small kobold at "speed +1": monsters get a random spread of
   about one point (`mon-make.c:1158-1164`), hence `fast_fragile_speed_margin`.
   Group counting is by same-race monsters in view (recent, `BORG_VIEW`), a
   hypothesis; the issue's corridor/stairs question for a real swarm is not
   built.

### After the review (OpenCode, MiniMax-M3, #847, `7f8c6b80`)

- **Phase Door through `phase_door()`** (finding 1): the first version read the
  scroll or cast the spell directly, outside the landing test, the shipped #584
  rule and the anti-summon delay. It is now `PhaseSite::FastFragile`
  with a row in `docs/PHASE-DOOR.md`; the #584 rule (an awake enemy adjacent and
  the fight lost) and the anti-summon gate now apply. **The landing test is
  behind `fast_fragile_phase_landing`, shipped 0.** Routed with the emergency
  landing test the policy left **4 of 8** dead (was 1): at HP 3–6 against a
  crow's danger of 18 every landing grid is scary (100 of 100) and the test
  refused the Phase Door three times where it had saved the Gnome Druid, the
  Necromancer and the Half-Elf Druid; the #584 rule allowed all of them. The
  trade is the owner's: the landing test is the core's safeguard against a bad
  random landing; the crow is twice as fast and finds the Borg anywhere, so the
  Phase Door's value is the free shots. The zoo measures both (the
  `named-landing` arm).
- **The ban in the zoo arms** (finding 2): with the policy on, `*-ban` equals its
  non-ban arm (the attack comes first). The request now says so and adds the
  ban-alone arm.
- **Dropped `unknown_damage` veto** (finding 3): deliberate, now written in
  CALIBRATION.md.
- Finding 4 (#747's 50% against the 25% mark) and finding 5 (per-think cost, no
  cache): follow-up issues, not in this PR.
- The invented example note in CALIBRATION.md was replaced by a real one; the
  parameter count was stale.

### What was not done

  the PR. No claim of improvement (RULES 14).
- The issue's **"no Desperate for Stairs walk from a faster monster"** (item 4):

  policy on, the attack comes first.

  damage decides (finding 5), not a family order.
- fde8025a-like deaths by cave spiders, centipedes and bats were not read:
  they need the zoo's population (`fast_fragile_by_stats`).
- A cornered Borg with no cure and no escape just attacks.
