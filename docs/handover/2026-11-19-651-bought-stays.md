## 2026-11-19: what a town visit bought is not stored in the Home (#651)

From borg-cemetery#75: the CL1 Priest 1569794094 bought a Cure Light Wounds, stored it in the Home in the same visit, and bought another. It was 20 gold short later.

- **Cause:** the town plan's store rule (`borg_town_utility.rs`, `dispositions`) stores a stack when its value at Home for later visits beats its value carried (`future - home_cost > loss - cost`). The "bought this visit" flag (`just_bought`) kept a stack from being sold or dropped, but not from being stored.
- **Fix:** from plan schema 12 (`PLAN_SCHEMA`, `borg_town_record.rs`; records of schema 11 are still read), a stack bought this visit is never stored. Records of earlier schemas replay their original decision.
  - `borg_town_record.rs` is now a declared hooked file (`scripts/borg-core-vendor.py`).
  - Unit test: `what_this_visit_bought_is_not_stored`.
- **No parameter:** it is a bug fix.
- **The Priest:** it keeps its first CLW and does not buy a second. With the 20 gold it buys 7 flasks of oil, and it survives (CL4, DL2) where main's game dies.
- **classes-18 at 100,000 turns, main `13e1187c` against the fix:**
  - same-visit buy-then-store: 12 → 0;
  - deaths 1 → 2, sum of CL 101 → 100, sum of max DL 37 → 40;
  - 13 of 18 games change.
  - The deaths: the Priest is saved; a Druid (poison, out of CLW) and a Rogue (soldier, with 16 CLW in the pack) newly die. Neither was traced; both are games that took another path.
  - At 18 games this is direction only; a zoo pilot is the measure.
- **Smoke:** 2 of 3 games change (priest-3 from turn 651, warrior-1 from 1979). `smoke-borg-rs` was re-recorded with `scripts/rerecord-smoke-borg-rs.sh`, its first use in commit mode.
