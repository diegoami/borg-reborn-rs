## 2026-11-22: grey mold worlds x4 and x8 (#1022)

**Done.** I added two worlds, `harness/worlds/molds-x4.txt` and `molds-x8.txt`. Each one multiplies every other race's rarity by 4 or 8 through #970's `favour = grey mold`. No code changed, and nothing changes outside a world.

The purpose is to make the #975 case more frequent: a monster left alive at exactly 0 HP (`mon_take_hit` kills only below 0, `mon-util.c:1300`). The grey mold suits it:
- **HP:** about 2 ± 1 (`mon_hp`, `mon-make.c:956`), so one arrow or dagger hit often leaves it at exactly 0.
- **Depth:** DL1.
- **Danger:** it never moves and spores for 1d4 twice a turn.
- **No confusion:** unlike #969's grey mushroom patch.
