## 2026-11-22: A miss is not a wall, and no wall is guessed under the Borg (#929)

All checks ran on the default core, **borg-rs** (`borg-core/`, `harness/borg.txt`).

**Cause (confirmed at `4b2c2fcf`).** After a ranged attack with no hit message,
`borg_update` (`borg-core/src/borg_update.rs:1735`) calls
`borg_target_unknown_wall` (`borg_projection.rs:783`, C
`borg-projection.c:739-830`). The walk starts at the Borg's own grid (C
:753-754). With no unknown grid it reaches the target, steps back once
(C :823) and writes granite there (C :826). For an adjacent target, that
grid is the Borg's own. A thrown miss prints nothing (`make_ranged_throw`,
`player-attack.c:1278-1280`), and the missile still reaches the monster's grid
(`ranged_helper` stops only before an impassable, non-projectable grid,
:1101-1108). So a miss at a monster in view never means a wall, and an adjacent
one never can.

**Fix, shipped 0:** `update_miss_wall_guess`. With 1, a miss at a monster in
view that was seen at the shot guesses nothing, and `successful_target`
becomes 2 as today. No guess writes the Borg's grid either: the walk
(`wall_guess_walk`, now a pure function) starts at the first step. With 0,
play is C's. Unit tests: `wall_guess_never_lands_on_the_borg`
(borg_projection.rs) and `a_miss_at_a_monster_in_view_is_not_a_wall`
(borg_update.rs). The deviation is recorded in
`docs/c-borg/borg-rs-c-bugs.md` §41 and in CALIBRATION.md.

**What the granite did (answers to the issue's questions).**
- Flows, danger and target choice, in these games: nothing. A temporary probe
  (not committed) showed that the Borg's grid was remembered at all 7
  guesses. The map pass of the same `borg_update` put back the floor, or the
  open door the Paladin stood in, before any think read it. Side effects of
  that wall↔floor flip: flow data reset for the grid, the view and light
  recomputed, and `wizard_light`/`detect_walls` zeroed. The timers were
  already 0 here; in a game that had cast them, the flip would make the Borg
  light or detect again early.
- It persists only when the player does not remember its own grid (light 0,
  the Necromancer 798380952). Then `spread_blocked` (`borg_flow.rs:428`) keeps
  every flow off the Borg's grid, and `borg_projectable`
  (`borg_projection.rs:349`) reads every path *to* the Borg as blocked, which
  hides ranged danger. This comes from reading the source; the case was not
  replayed.
- Does a miss at a visible monster in range mean a wall? No (see above). The
  remaining edge is a monster in view whose projection path clips a corner. That
  wall is a known grid, so an "unknown wall" guess adds nothing there either.
- `successful_target = 2` and `missed_before`: C's guess always returns true,
  so a throw's -12 ("skip only one round") is never reached from this path.
  That cancellation lets the Borg throw again at once twice in these games
  (Paladin 1621 after the miss at 1611; Druid 2382 after 2372). With 1 the
  round is still not skipped, after a miss in view or after any guess
  (`NextToBorg` included: the first version left -11 after a spell there, a
  lockout until the Borg moves; fixed after the review of #1005).

**Bears on #986 (other session; its branch not touched).** The wall guess does
not change either Fang fight: the same keys with the fix on. The guess's map
write cannot explain the throw misses or the "winning" race. Every miss there
was an ordinary `test_hit` failure at distance 1. The round that
`missed_before` would skip after a miss is always cancelled, so the Borg
re-throws at once.
