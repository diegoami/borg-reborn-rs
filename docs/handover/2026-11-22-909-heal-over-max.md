# 2026-11-22 — #909: Cure Light Wounds when danger ≥ max HP, and the outmatched heal before the walk

**Issue:** diegoami/borg-reborn-rs#909. **Branch:** `task/T909-heal-over-max`,
stacked on #908 ← #907. Track P, P7.

## What was done

Two switches, compiled and shipped 0:

- **`caution_heal_lethal_over_max`.** `borg_heal`'s lethal bonus
  (`caution_heal_lethal_chance_bonus`, 75) also applies when the danger is at
  or above the max HP (`lethal_heal_bonus_applies`, unit-tested).
- **`caution_heal_outmatched_before_walk`.** While fleeing or on a scary
  level, `borg_heal_outmatched` (#457) is tried just before "Desperation Head
  for stairs". Today it is only tried at the end of `borg_caution`.

## What was found

Rogue 381988580 at 62,899: HP 23/35, danger 41, 21 CLW.

- **The skip.** The CLW test passes, but the band-3 skip is certain
  (chance ≤ 74 < 80) because 41 ≥ 35 removes the bonus. In the
  counterfactual, a CLW there survives.
- **The later turns.** The danger exceeds HP + 20, so the regular CLW refuses.
- **The outmatched heal.** It would apply from 51% lost (62,909), but it was
  behind the walk.
- The case is in borg-cemetery `runs/2026-11-22-fastfrag-rogue-381988580`.
