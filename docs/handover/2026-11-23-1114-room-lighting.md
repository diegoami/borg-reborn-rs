## #1114 Room-lighting spells on revisits

### What changed

Track P. The radius-one room check still allows the first eligible cast
from each square, including when a nearby patch is already glowing. The
Mage's Light Room and the Priest's and Paladin's Call Light both flood
the room through `LIGHT_AREA` and can reveal dark floor beyond their
spell projections.

After the engine confirms either spell, the Borg remembers the dark
floor it can still observe from that square. A revisit can cast again
if it sees new dark floor. Unconfirmed casts leave the square eligible,
and a level change clears the record. This covers discretionary lighting
during exploration and before rest.

### Verification

The decision tests cover the mixed-glow first cast, newly observed dark
floor, and unconfirmed or displaced casts. A regression for each spell
runs the shared cast decision, success confirmation and observation
before returning to the same square.

In the native Priest smoke run (seed 103), the first cast from
(49,136) still succeeds at turn 411. On the revisit at 1161, the Borg
keeps walking and casts from the next eligible square at 1171. The
other three smoke runs match the previous reference.

The original recording was unavailable, so the decision regressions use
synthetic observations. No class-wide gameplay comparison was run.
