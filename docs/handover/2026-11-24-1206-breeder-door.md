## 2026-11-24: The breeder containment closes the door the flight opened (#1206)

Track P, branch `task/T1206-breeder-door`, target `track-p`.
Based on `track-p` at `20ac7d19`.

### What was done

- The defect: on breeder levels, `borg_play_step`'s containment
  (`close_breeder_door`) skips only the **currently selected** next grid.
  The flow replans after each terrain change, so the just-opened door
  loses its goal-side protection and the containment closes it again; the
  next replan opens it. The recorded Kobold Mage `Borg261124194229`
  (CL19, DL4, danger 0, HP 105, leaving and fleeing set) alternated
  `# Opening a door` / `# Closing a door` 35 times — 70 actions from one
  square — between turns 3,638,689 and 3,639,379.
- The fix (the issue's "suppress an immediate undo within the same
  flight", scoped per the review of PR #1207): `step_closed_door` records
  each door it opens as `(x, y, turn)` in `st.flow.opened_doors` (at most
  8 kept; cleared on a new level) **only while the escape context is
  armed** (`goal.fleeing || goal.leaving` — the recorded flight had both
  set), and `close_breeder_door` skips an adjacent open door from that
  record while it is younger than `OPENED_DOOR_UNDO_TURNS` (30 turns —
  the recorded cycle ran ten turns per toggle) **and the context is still
  armed**. So a door opened by ordinary play (exploration) is never
  exempt, and a deliberate containment becomes eligible as soon as the
  flight's context ends or the window expires. Unrelated open doors still
  close. `borg_play_step` is `pub(crate)` so the fixture can drive the
  step directly.
- The regression (`breeder-door-loop`, `BreederDoorLoopTest`, CI wired),
  per `docs/DECISION-FIXTURES.md`'s recipe: a fabricated breeder level —
  walls everywhere, a carved corridor whose only passage is the flight
  path's closed door, an unrelated open door in a niche beside it (later
  in the containment's scan order), the down stairs beyond — driven turn
  by turn through `borg_play_step` with **adjacent next grids**, as the
  flows hand them, applying the door state after each action **along the
  direction the Borg queued** (`borg_queue_direction`) and advancing the
  turn for every action. Asserts: the containment closes the unrelated
  door; the flight path's door opens **exactly once** and is never closed
  again during the flight; the Borg walks past the door; and afterwards a
  deliberate containment closes that door once the context ends **and**
  once the window expires (two separate phases).
