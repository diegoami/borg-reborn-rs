## 2026-11-24: The breeder containment closes the door the flight opened (#1206)

Track P, branch `task/T1206-breeder-door`, target `track-p`.
Based on `track-p` at `20ac7d19`.

### What was done

- The defect: on breeder levels, `borg_play_step`'s containment
  (`close_breeder_door`) skips only the **currently selected** next grid.
  The flow replans after each terrain change, so the just-opened door
  loses its goal-side protection and the containment closes it again; the
  next replan opens it. The recorded Kobold Mage `Borg261124194229`
  (CL19, DL4, danger 0, HP 105, leaving and fleeing set) alternated
  `# Opening a door` / `# Closing a door` 35 times — 70 actions from one
  square — between turns 3,638,689 and 3,639,379.
- The fix (the issue's "suppress an immediate undo within the same
  flight", scoped per the three review rounds of PR #1207):
  `step_closed_door` records each door it opens as `(x, y, turn)` in
  `st.flow.opened_doors` (at most 8 kept; cleared on a new level) **only
  while the escape context is armed** (`goal.fleeing || goal.leaving` —
  the recorded flight had both set), and `close_breeder_door` skips an
  adjacent open door from that record while it is younger than
  `OPENED_DOOR_UNDO_TURNS` (30 turns — the recorded cycle ran ten turns
  per toggle), the context is still armed, **and no disarm has been
  observed since the open**. The disarm is observed at the transitions
  themselves, not at the containment: `escape_context_tick` runs at the
  start of every dungeon think (`borg_think_dungeon`), and the two
  scare-goal withdrawal sites in `borg_caution` stamp
  `escape_context_disarm_now` directly — a scare can be withdrawn and
  re-armed within one think, so the flags never read off across a think
  boundary. `FlowState.escape_last_disarm` carries the stamp (reset on a
  new level). So an exemption is a property of one continuous armed run:
  an ordinary open is never exempt, a deliberate containment works as
  soon as the flight's context ends — even when the containment never
  got a turn in between — and no exemption carries into a new flight.
  (Review 2's containment-scoped clear is superseded by the
  transition-level rule: any think it could wipe on, the tick already
  invalidated the entries.) Unrelated open doors still close.
  `borg_play_step` is `pub(crate)` so the fixture can drive the step
  directly.
- The regression (`breeder-door-loop`, `BreederDoorLoopTest`, CI wired),
  per `docs/DECISION-FIXTURES.md`'s recipe: a fabricated breeder level —
  walls everywhere, a carved corridor whose only passage is the flight
  path's closed door, an unrelated open door in a niche beside it (later
  in the containment's scan order), the down stairs beyond — driven turn
  by turn through `borg_play_step` with **adjacent next grids**, as the
  flows hand them, ticking the escape context at every think, and
  applying the door state after each action **along the direction the
  Borg queued** (`borg_queue_direction`) and advancing the turn for
  every action. Five phases: (1) the flight — the corridor door opens
  **exactly once**, is never closed again while the Borg walks past it,
  and the niche door stays closed; (2) the context ends — the
  containment closes the corridor door; (3) route A — a new flight's own
  open, the flight ends with only the tick observing the disarm (no
  containment turn), ordinary play reopens the door, a third flight
  re-arms inside the old window and must close it; (4) route B — a
  mid-think scare withdrawal (`escape_context_disarm_now`, the flags
  never read off) and the re-armed scare must close the door; (5) the
  new flight's own open ages out of the window while armed and
  continuous — the containment closes it.
