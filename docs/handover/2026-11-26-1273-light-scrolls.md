## 2026-11-26 — Spend or discard Scrolls of Light (#1273)

Necromancers ignore the kind when they carry a known stack. Angband drops
the stack and filters future copies out of native pickup. The Borg skips
known floor copies in both item flows and excludes them from town
purchases and Home withdrawals.

Other classes spend a carried scroll on a quiet, unglowing dungeon
square, even by torchlight in a corridor. The read precedes ordinary
lighting and uses the same confirmed lighting history as room spells.
Combat, retreat and Recall retain priority. Excess scrolls can still be
dropped as part of ordinary inventory management.

All four shipped smoke runs matched the existing reference. A native
check with six known scrolls in the birth kit confirmed that the
Necromancer dropped all six and the Warrior used all six while
exploring dark squares. The saved incident was not replayed.

The PR review found two exact stair-handoff replays changed route.
They pin `light_scroll_idle_spend = 0` to restore their original
lighting and junk consumption. The compiled default is 0 and the
Track P profile enables the requested dark-square spending at 1.
Native regression tests now cover whole-stack discard, use on
successive dark squares, combat priority and the off setting.
