## 2026-11-21 · Short-gold town shopping (#828)

The default borg-rs core has an opt-in short-gold policy. Start from the
shipped profile with `borg_town_plan = 1`, keep
`borg_param_town_missiles_percent = -1`, and set
`borg_param_town_short_gold = 1` on the candidate only. The same source
commit with `borg_param_town_short_gold = 0` is the control.

The town planner values food and light, one Phase Door, one healing
potion, and up to five throwable flasks in that order. Lantern fuel
uses its own allocation. A ready ranged spell, compatible ammunition
with an equipped launcher, or a charged attack device can cover the
ranged need. When oil is unavailable, the planner can value throwable
iron shot or a complete launcher and ammunition purchase. The throw
scorer protects two throwables after fuel and checks the projected
impact target before spending either. The fast-threat and immobile
escape tests are pilot heuristics.

Validation includes the native shopping step at several gold limits,
carried supplies, a matching launcher pair, and threat classification.
The paired all-class pilot and any resulting tuning are tracked in
[borg-zoo #575](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-zoo/issues/575).
