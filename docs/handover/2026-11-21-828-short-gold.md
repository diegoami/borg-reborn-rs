## 2026-11-21 · Short-gold town shopping (#828)

The default borg-rs core has an opt-in short-gold policy. Start from the
shipped profile with `borg_town_plan = 1`, keep
`borg_param_town_missiles_percent = -1`, and set
`borg_param_town_short_gold = 1` on the candidate only. The same source
commit with `borg_param_town_short_gold = 0` is the control.

The town planner values food and light, one Phase Door, one healing
potion, and up to five throwable flasks in that order. Lantern fuel
uses its own allocation. A ready ranged spell, compatible ammunition
with an equipped launcher, or a charged attack device can cover the
ranged need. When oil is unavailable, the planner can value throwable
iron shot or a complete launcher and ammunition purchase. The throw
scorer protects two throwables after fuel and checks the projected
impact target before spending either. The fast-threat and immobile
escape tests are pilot heuristics.

Validation includes the native shopping step at several gold limits,
carried supplies, a matching launcher pair, and threat classification.
The paired all-class pilot and any resulting tuning are tracked in
[borg-zoo #723](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-zoo/issues/723), which
supersedes [borg-zoo #575](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-zoo/issues/575)
(its pin `67f1af15` never ran).

### 2026-11-23: synchronized with `track-p`, Codex review fixes

`track-p` (`c687334c`) is merged in. Its new capability rows (Recall
reserve, prepared remedies, staples, Restore Life reserve, first cure,
#895's device rows: ids 63-81) push the pilot's rows after them:
FIRST_HEAL 82, FIRST_PHASE 83, LAUNCHER 84-86, LAUNCHER_AMMO 87-89 (90
names). With the pilot off the model still stops at FIRST_HEAL (padded
by first cures or devices), so the shipped facts keep their shape.

The Codex review (PR #854) found, and this fixes:

- F1: town classified oil as fuel against the 20,000-turn fuel goal while
  the pilot's light requirement and combat used the trip length, so a
  lantern with 8,000 turns left on a 4,000-turn trip saw every flask as
  fuel and, an affordable flask closing the fallback, bought neither oil
  nor shot. Town, the pilot's light requirement and combat's held-oil count
  now share `fuel_goal` and `fuel_bottles_needed`. Shot and a launcher are
  the fallback when no affordable flask would be left to throw after fuel.
- F2: the pilot replaced the configured Phase Door reserve (6; Ranger and
  Blackguard 10) with one scroll. The first scroll is now a row of its own
  (FIRST_PHASE); the configured reserve holds in both arms.
- F3: the held-oil gate compared a cheaper throw's raw average with the
  target's HP. Both throws are now rated as the scorer rates them
  (`rated_throw`: brand, point blank, hit chance) and the cheaper one must
  reach the target (`throw_range`).
- F4: any charged attack wand or ready rod filled the ranged reserve; it now
  must pass the bolt attacks' failure limit (`short_gold_device_usable`,
  `device_failure_of`, which treats a level 90 or more above the skill as
  never working instead of dividing by zero).

The native pilot (#723) is still to run; the default stays 0.
