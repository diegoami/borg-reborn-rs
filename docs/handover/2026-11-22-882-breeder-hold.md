## 2026-11-22: Slow breeders: hold, or cut the known way out (#882)

The owner put #882 on track P (2026-11-22). The rule is a switch of its
own, `caution_breeder_hold`, read at every value of `caution_breeder_policy`
and **off at the shipped settings**. The first commits made it layer 6 of the
policy; after reviewing the 0–5 ladder the owner chose a separate switch
(2026-11-22, option 2 on PR #893), so it can go on over the shipped policy 3
alone. The policy is back to -1..5.

The same change fixes **layer 5** (#544's breeder exit). Its exit grid was any
chokepoint. Every grid of a checkerboard room is one (its floor neighbours are
diagonal and do not touch), so the exit could walk to a grid inside the room.
It now takes `room_exit_grid` (a chokepoint with an orthogonal passable
neighbour), which the hold uses too. The unseen-monster walk-away (#697) keeps
`breeder_exit_grid` as it was.

### What was done

- A new borg-core module of ours, `borg-core/src/borg_breeder_hold.rs`
  (listed in `scripts/borg-core-vendor.py` OURS). It is called once in
  `borg_caution`, after the heals, defence and critical needs and before every
  movement rule: the flight to the stairs, the breeder and crowd exits,
  retreat, the escapes and back-away.
- **When it fires.** Every awake monster within `caution_breeder_hold_scan_radius`
  (8) is a breeder:
  - none has a ranged attack or spell (`ranged_attack`);
  - none is faster than the Borg, and none is as fast unless erratic (`RAND_25`/`RAND_50`);
  - one is closer than `caution_breeder_exit_distance` (6).

  And the colony is big (`caution_breeder_hold_count`, 5 at first, now 1: see below), growing
  (`caution_breeder_hold_growth` 2 more than the fewest seen earlier within
  `caution_breeder_hold_growth_turns` 100 game turns; first sight is not
  growth), or has lost or scared the Borg off the level.
- **What it does.**
  - **Cuts out.** It walks the cheapest route over known grids only (an
    unknown grid is a wall, #881) to a known staircase, or to a corridor or
    doorway clear of every known breeder. A corridor or doorway is a chokepoint
    with an orthogonal floor neighbour, so the grids of a checkerboard room,
    which touch only diagonally, are not exits.

    Each breeder on the route costs `caution_breeder_hold_worm_cost` (3) more
    steps. The Borg attacks the breeder on the route (`+` and a direction) and
    steps otherwise. The cut-out needs at most `caution_breeder_hold_max_on_route`
    (8) breeders on the route, and HP at least `caution_breeder_hold_go_hp_percent`
    (40), or `caution_breeder_hold_cure_hp_percent` (25) with a CLW/CSW/CCW potion.
  - **Walks on.** From the exit it walks the known route on to the nearest
    known staircase and takes it (`breeder_hold_stair`). While only slow
    breeders are about, the walk owns the movement (#556).
  - **Holds** otherwise. It attacks the weakest adjacent breeder, or stays
    still (`,`) for at most `caution_breeder_hold_idle_turns` (50) idle thinks
    in a row.
  - **Yields** to the other rules with a free staircase within
    `caution_breeder_easy_escape_steps`, or HP below
    `caution_breeder_hold_floor_hp_percent` (25) with no cure potion.
- **Settings and parameters.** Eleven parameters in `params.rs`, a block in
  `harness/borg.txt` and rows in `docs/CALIBRATION.md`. Every number is a
  hypothesis (RULES 14), taken from the issue. The registry test accepts the
  `caution_breeder_hold*` parameters citing their own module.
- **Unit tests.** The firing test, growth (first sight is not growth), the HP
  margin, and routes in a checkerboard room. The routes cover the doorway as
  the only exit, a worm on the only way out, and unknown grids as walls.
