# Repurpose junk_gear_alternatives as Home backups per slot

Issue #1310, stacked on #1309 (PR #1313). Last of four fixes from the
equipment routine audit of `8d85f44f`.

## Found

Under the shipped Home backup policy `junk_gear_alternatives` changed
nothing outside diggers:

- `backup_counts` kept the worn item, one regular backup per slot (two for
  rings) and a lighter option.
- The unresolved paths hard-coded two alternatives.
- The YAML text "Choose 3 for more backups" was wrong, and a second
  distinct role was always released.

## Done (owner decision, 2026-11-27)

- **New meaning.** With `town_gear_home_backups` on, `junk_gear_alternatives`
  is the number of regular backups per slot, each a pair for rings. The
  shipped value and the compiled default go from 2 to 1. At 1 the plan
  keeps exactly what it kept before.
- **Extra backups.** A backup beyond the first stays only if it supplies a
  known property that no worn piece and no better kept backup gives. It
  stays only at Home, or in the pack while the remembered Home has a free
  stack. Town cleanup then stores it.
- **Diggers.** Diggers keep the earlier cutoff of two better options
  (`UNPLANNED_GEAR_CUTOFF`). The unresolved gear rules keep their
  two-alternative evidence.
- **Cutoff policy.** With `town_gear_home_backups` 0 the value is still the
  #1276 cutoff. That policy shipped with 2, so a replay of it should now set
  `borg_param_junk_gear_alternatives: 2` explicitly.
  `engine/tests/check_spare_gear.py` already does.
- **Citations.** Two parameter citations in `borg_junk.rs` moved, because
  the new helpers sit above them.

## Review fix (owner, PR #1314)

An extra backup is tested against the combined known properties of the worn
piece and the kept backups, not against each piece alone. With worn Free
Action boots and a kept rNexus backup, boots that give both no longer take
an extra slot. Drawbacks are left out of the combined properties.

A second review found that, under randarts, a partly known worn artifact
still counted toward that combined coverage. A fully known extra whose
property only that piece seemed to give could be released. Untrusted pieces
(#1313's `trusted`) now stay worn or kept but cover nothing here.

## Checked

- Release unit test `extra_home_backups_need_a_distinct_property_and_home_room`:
  - the rNexus boots beside Free Action boots leave at 1;
  - they stay at Home at 2;
  - a carried extra needs Home room;
  - a plain third backup leaves at either value;
  - boots whose properties the worn piece and the kept backup cover together
    leave;
  - the fully known extra stays when only an untrusted worn randart appears
    to cover its property.
- The native gear and town tests pass at the shipped value.
- The shipped smoke suite is identical to `smoke-borg-rs`.
  `scripts/rerecord-smoke-borg-rs.sh`, which AGENTS.md asks for after a
  shipped value changes, found the games identical and recorded nothing
  new.
