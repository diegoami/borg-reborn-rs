## 2026-11-28: At most two spare melee weapons, at Home and in the pack (#1416)

### What was done

- `town_home_melee_spares` (compiled default 0, shipped 2 in
  `harness/pborg.yaml`, `docs/CALIBRATION.md`; owner decision on #1416,
  2026-11-28) caps the spare melee weapons kept **in all**, at Home and in
  the pack together. It acts inside `borg_spare_gear::gear_plan` for the
  weapon slot, under the ranked Home backup plan (`town_gear_home_backups`),
  so one plan serves the town cleanup (`organize_gear`: a Home spare is sold,
  or dropped when no shop buys it) and the dungeon drop
  (`planned_pack_excess`, on a quiet turn under the safe-drop checks).
- The spares rank by `GearChoice::power`, then the lighter weapon, then the
  location (`cap_spares`). The best spares stay.
- **No artifact exception** (owner decision 2): `protected_gear`'s unlimited
  release of an artifact or a known activation no longer applies to melee
  weapons while the cap is on. They count and rank like any spare.
- The #1316 protection exemption does not apply to weapons while the cap is
  on (`backup_counts` gets no worn-kit protections for the weapon slot).
- Still never released, but counted: the active weapon swap, a staged Home
  loadout (`retained_home_gear`), a weapon with an inscription (`{`) and a
  partly known weapon the plan only observes. If these alone exceed the cap
  nothing else stays, and they are not released.
- Tests: `the_melee_cap_ranks_by_power_then_weight` (unit);
  `melee_cap_fixture` (native, `BORG_TEST_EXPLORATION_PACK=melee-cap`,
  `engine/tests/test_exploration_pack.py`): with three Home spares and a
  lowest-power artifact in the pack, a cap of 2 keeps the best Home spare and
  releases the artifact (3 and 4 spares stay at cap 0); in the pack alone, a
  quiet-turn drop names the artifact (`ranked_pack_spare`).

### What was found

- The #1310 count (`junk_gear_alternatives`) already limited ordinary spares
  to the backup and the lighter alternative, so at the shipped values the cap
  bites on artifacts, activation weapons and the extras that
  `junk_gear_alternatives` above 1 or the #1316 exemption would keep. The
  lighter alternative can now leave when two stronger spares outrank it; that
  is the owner's rule 2 (rank by power). A shipped-value zoo comparison
  (Home composition per visit from `BORG_TOWN_DUMP`) has not run.
- The cap is not read with `town_gear_home_backups` 0 (the older redundancy
  path stays as it was).

### What is left, and owner decisions

- Weapons with unknown runes (one per slot by #1308) are not counted in the
  cap; say if they should be.
- The ordering is by power alone. If the lighter alternative should outrank
  an artifact, that is a different rule.

### Review of the design (Codex, GPT-6, issue comment 7245374894)

- P2, zero-gold sales: fixed. `spare_sale` and `release_home_gear` took a
  weapon to a shop only for a positive quote, so with `birth_no_selling` (the
  default, quote 0) an over-cap weapon was dropped. A zero quote now counts as
  accepted under `birth_no_selling` (`sale_accepted`); the shop must still buy
  that kind (`borg_store_buys`) and have room (`store_accepts_quantity`). This
  applies to every ranked-plan release, not only the cap, so it also changes
  where other redundant spares go (sold, not dropped). Not covered by a test
  that runs the sale: it needs a game.
- P1, the active swap: it counts toward the two and is never released
  (`pinned` in `cap_spares`), so a swap feeding the DL40 poison gate stays;
  the plan's upgrade piece is outside the count. Unit-tested with a swap
  distinct from the top-ranked spares. The case with one Home and two pack
  spares and a distinct swap in a native game is not built.
- P3: shipped 2 is an immediate exception on the owner's decision of
  2026-11-28 (RULES 28), not the P9 activation. Control: `town_home_melee_spares`
  0; candidate: 2.
