# One rule to buy, keep and drink the stat-trade potions

Issue #1371. A level 20 Elf Ranger carried a Potion of Nimbleness for 1.7
million game turns and bought a Potion of Contemplation at the Black Market
for 3,000 gold. The evidence is in
[Borg Cemetery PR #165](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-cemetery/pull/165).

## Found

- **No intentional use.** `borg_use_things` drinks only the five pure stat
  potions. The gain-one/lose-one potions (Brawn, Intellect, Contemplation,
  Nimbleness, Toughness) were drunk only by `borg_consume`, when a pack-space
  or weight cleanup picked the stack. `borg_junk`'s class table already lets
  a Ranger drink Nimbleness (and Brawn and Toughness). Nothing called it.
- **Contemplation was bought as a Wisdom gain.** The knowledge bit is set on
  the `{unseen}` offer (`borg_town_shadow.rs`, `serves`). However,
  `borg_town_w_knowledge` is forced to 0 in the plan (`effective_params`,
  #460), so learning the flavour was worth nothing. The planner valued the
  stat row only. The class filter for these offers,
  `town_second_stat_potions_by_class`, ships 0.
- **The engine trade** (`player.c:player_stat_inc`, `player_stat_dec`;
  `effect_handler_LOSE_RANDOM_STAT`): the potion restores, then gains its
  stat. It permanently lowers one of the four other stats, each with chance
  1/4. Sustains do not stop the loss. On the table scale (a point below 18
  is a step, ten points of 18/xx are one), the expected gain is 26.5
  tenths at 18/00, 14.5 at 18/50 and 10.5 at 18/66. From 18/67 it falls
  under one step, except at exactly 18/90. A loss is one step (10 tenths)
  above 18/10.

## Done

`town_stat_trade_policy` (default 0, the earlier play; shipped 1 in
`harness/pborg.yaml`) makes one answer,
`borg_town_capability::second_stat_potion_wanted`, drive every path. A
character wants the potion when its class drinks it (the `borg_junk` table)
and `E[gain] × w_stat ≥ margin × E[Σ w_lost × loss] / 4`, in table tenths,
at its permanent stats (`stat_max`; review below).

- **Shop:** an offer it refuses is worth nothing in every shop, flavour
  learned or not (`supplies_of_kind`). This replaces
  `town_second_stat_potions_by_class` while on.
- **Disposal:** a known pack or Home copy it refuses is `unwanted_home_item`.
  It is sold or dropped in town and cleared from Home through #1049's path.
  An unknown copy is refused once identification discloses its kind.
  `unwanted_home_item` now takes the state.
- **Intentional use:** `borg_use_things` drinks a wanted copy when the
  danger here is at most a tenth of current HP. That is the quiet turn of
  `borg_discard_town_consumable`.
- **Junk cleanup:** `borg_consume` drinks only a wanted copy. It no longer
  drinks a refused one to destroy it.
- **Floor and dungeon pack** (`town_stat_trade_floor`, shipped 1):
  `borg_flow_take` skips a floor object of a known stat-trade potion the
  character refuses, and `borg_junk::borg_discard_refused_stack` drops (ignores,
  this stack only) one that the engine's `pickup_always` put in the pack, on a
  quiet dungeon turn. The rest of the never-used list (#443) is not filtered:
  Treasure Detection and the junk potions have dungeon uses.
- **Parameters:** `town_stat_trade_margin_percent` (100) and
  `town_stat_trade_weight_{str,int,wis,dex,con}` (100), per class with
  `__<class>`. All are hypotheses: no measurement chose them.

The Ranger cases are unit tests (`borg_town_capability.rs`,
`recall_value_tests`). At Dexterity 18/50 with Strength 18/40, Wisdom 18/20
and Constitution 18/30, Nimbleness is worthwhile. At 18/100 the gain is
capped. At 18/95 the gain is 5 tenths against a whole step. With double
weights on Strength, Wisdom (the Ranger's casting stat, `realm.txt`:
nature) and Constitution, the losses outweigh the gain at 18/50.
Contemplation is refused by the class table.

The native case is `engine/tests/test_junk_consumables.py`,
`test_town_drinks_the_stat_trade_it_accepts_and_sells_the_one_it_refuses`.
It uses the shipped CI build `test-junk-consumables`. The synthetic build
accepts only a Human Warrior, so this case uses one, not a Ranger. With the
policy on, the Warrior drinks Nimbleness on its first `borg_use_things`. It
sells Contemplation and the Second Sight mushrooms and keeps its two Cure
Light Wounds. With the policy off, both potions stay.

## Review (Codex, `2f70ab1b`) and the changes after it

- **P1:** the rule reads `stat_max`, not the drained `stat_cur`: the potion
  restores before `GAIN_STAT` and the loss lowers another stat's maximum. A
  pure variant, `second_stat_potion_wanted_at`, carries the arithmetic; the
  unit tests include the drained-at-cap Ranger (18/90 from 18/100) and a
  drained Strength that hid a table step of loss.
- **P2:** the floor filter no longer covers the whole `WORTHLESS` list. It is
  the five stat-trade potions only. The CI failure
  (`test_known_up_route_continues_past_adjacent_down_stair`) was this: the
  Borg routed around a known Treasure Detection stack, then picked it up
  anyway.
- **P3:** the dungeon-side drop above, with a native case
  (`test_dungeon_drops_a_refused_stat_potion_the_engine_picked_up`).
- **P8:** `town_stat_trade_floor` is its own switch, and RULES 28 (owner,
  2026-11-28: "we do not have time to wait for zoo ... we have no customers
  or rollouts") says a fix ships on in its PR. Both ship 1. The effect is not
  measured and the PR claims no improvement (RULES 14).

## For the owner

- Contemplation stays refused for Rangers by `borg_junk`'s table, as the
  issue says. Wisdom is the Ranger's casting stat. Allowing it is a one-line
  change to `drinks_second_stat_potion`.
- Per-class weights, for example Constitution above 100 for every class, are
  a zoo question.
