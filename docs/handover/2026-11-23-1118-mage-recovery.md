## #1118 Mage recovery movement under the breeder rest veto

The recovery route now uses the same breeder policy as the rest it
seeks. When that policy says to hunt or leave, the Borg releases an
active recovery route and declines a new route. The ordinary path
still moves to a safe resting square when rest is permitted.

The constructed native regression uses a low-mana Mage. It starts
an ordinary recovery walk, introduces a known breeder, verifies that
the route is canceled without a movement key, and exercises new-route
rejection and later ordinary recovery once the breeder is gone.

The [canonical Cemetery case](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-cemetery/pull/139)
retains the exact stop save and the uninterrupted recording excerpts.
The save loads at the recorded stop, but the reported build's headless
reload stops before Borg decisions. The earlier autosave reaches its
cap on a fresh Borg. The constructed regression tests the decision
state that the uninterrupted trace demonstrated.

Review coverage now calls the full dungeon thinker with a prepared
recovery route. It verifies a continuation step before the veto and a
released route with no recovery step after the breeder is counted.
Restoring the old unchecked flow call makes the native test fail with
the recovery goal still active.
