## 2026-11-28: One teleport is enough for DL10; realistic depth gates; more Cure Critical Wounds (#1438)

Owner: "one usable long-range teleport should satisfy the DL10 escape
requirement", then "make the DL requirement realistic" and "buy more healing,
CSW and CCW in particular". The recorded case is the level 24 Kobold
Necromancer `Borg261128204027` (borg-cemetery PR 171): ample gold, one or two
Critical potions against a gate of three, and a Staff of Teleportation that
counted as one escape against a gate of two or three.

### What was done

Shipped values in `harness/pborg.yaml` (all hypotheses, not measured in the zoo):

| setting | was | now |
|---|---|---|
| `prepared_dl10_min_teleports` | 3 | 1 |
| `restock_dl10_min_teleports` | 2 | 1 |
| `restock_dl20_min_teleports` | 5 | 2 |
| `prepared_dl26_min_teleports` | 8 | 2 |
| `restock_dl36_min_teleport_or_teleport_level` | 3 | 2 |
| `prepared_dl21_min_basic_resists` (fire stays a separate check) | 2 | 1 |
| `prepared_dl26_min_resist_cold`, `_electricity`, `_acid` | 1 each | 0 each |
| `prepared_dl26_min_basic_resists` (new, `borg_prepared.rs`) | | 2 |

| `town_staple_ccw` | 2 | 4 |
| `town_staple_csw` (common, and every class) | 3-4 | 5-6 |
| `town_ccw_gate_floor` (new, `borg_town_capability.rs`) | | 1 |

`town_ccw_gate_floor`: from DL10 until CL30 the planner's Cure Critical Wounds
row wants at least `prepared_dl10_min_cure_critical` copies (3) as a
requirement, before the dear rows' "rich enough for Recall" gate
(`town_prepared_dear_after_recall`) counts. At the Recall decision in town, a
shortage is noted (`# Town: Cure Critical Wounds short of the DL10 floor, N of
M (weaker cures do not count)`); Cure Light and Serious never satisfy the
Critical gate.

The DL10 gate and restock check, the powerdive look-ahead and the town Recall
decision all read the two settings above through `borg_prepared` (the Recall
decision checks the actual destination, `town_recall_prepared_depth_percent`).

### What was found

- The shops sell no Scroll of Teleportation (`store.txt`, Alchemist); the
  Magic shop's Staff of Teleportation (about 2000-2700 gold) is "normal",
  not guaranteed, stock. Counts of 2, 3, 5 and 8 can only come from the
  dungeon, so town could not close them.
- A teleport staff counts 1, and its charges only when the activation failure
  is under 50% (`borg_trait.rs:2093`). A poor device user's staff is one escape
  whatever its charges: why the Necromancer's six charges did not pass 2.
- A teleport spell counts +1000, so casters with one never hit these gates.
  A Teleport Level scroll adds to both the teleport and Teleport Level
  counts.
- Cure Critical and Serious Wounds are Alchemist "normal" stock, so a
  visit can find none; only Cure Light Wounds is always there. The old targets
  (staple 2 plus a prepared row of 2 behind the Recall gate) could not reach
  the DL10 gate of 3, nor DL26's 12 Serious or Critical.
- Resists: no shop sells a permanent one, so "all three of cold, lightning,
  acid" at DL26 could only come from dungeon finds.

### How it was checked

Unit tests in `borg_prepared.rs` (zero, one scroll or staff, DL20, DL21, DL26,
DL36, and that Cure Serious does not cover the Critical gate) and
`borg_town_capability.rs` (the row's third copy); the shipped-values test in
`params.rs`; the engine case `one-escape` (and the changed `blocked`
note) of `TownRecallReadinessTest`. Not checked: any zoo measurement of the
new values; whether a Staff of Teleportation is actually bought for 1.
