## 2026-11-18: a fleeing Druid takes Fox Form (#551)

Owner: Druids never use Fox Form, though it suits a Borg that flees so
often. After the first version, the owner added: use the new speed, make the
step threshold a parameter, and once a fox, escape, or change back with an
action before fighting.

- **Done:** `escape_fox_form` (compiled and shipped 0), `escape_fox_form_fail`
  (25), `escape_fox_form_min_steps` (3) and `escape_fox_form_speed` (10):
  - a Druid casts Fox Form before a flight step;
  - it keeps the shape while it flees or the level is scary;
  - it changes back before a melee blow, and an item or a spell changes it
    back through the game's prompt;
  - `borg_danger` and the stand decision count the fox's speed.

  Details and the parameter table are in docs/CALIBRATION.md.
- **Found:** keeping the shape only while fleeing made the Borg revert and
  recast on every stand/flee alternation of #530 (110 casts in one game).
  Keeping it on a scary level fixed that. Seven blows were still struck as a
  fox, all steps into an unseen monster.
- **Evidence (a lead):** on 29 Druid starts, on against off: the same 3
  deaths (CL1-2, before any flight), sum of CL 161 to 156, max depth 54 to
  56. The 16 other classes play identical keys.
- **Review of #594:**
  - **The stale flag, fixed:** `borg_flush` now clears `fox_revert`. A
    flushed change back could have made the next item command change back
    without acting.
  - **The anticipatory stand bonus:** it is now a parameter of its own,
    `escape_fox_form_stand_speed` (10). At 0, 1 game of 29 changes.
  - **Unchanged:** none of the 29 games changed with these fixes.
- **Left:** a zoo pilot on Druid starts measures survival. A step into an
  unseen monster still strikes as a fox; changing back before every flight
  step would stop that at a cost (an owner decision).
- **Owner:** whether to ship 1, and the zoo pilot.
