## #807: cap the desperate stair walk by actual moves

DBorg oscillated on DL3 because the nearby-up-stair rule accepted a stair
at less than 20 grids across the map and then sent the weighted router on a
118-move walk around the walls. On the adjacent square ordinary flight
picked a 21-move walk in the opposite direction. The final uninterrupted
stretch was 388 alternating moves. The player stopped the game alive.

weighted desperate search, including detours. The unweighted path and the
ordinary weighted flight retain their existing budgets. The regression
model recreates the 118- and 21-move routes and shows that the 19-move limit
refuses the long route. The local retained save restarts with fresh Borg
memory and does not reproduce the live loop on either build.
