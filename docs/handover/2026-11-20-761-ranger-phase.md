**Findings**
- The planner does not buy unknown flavours to learn them: the
  buy-to-identify weight is 0 in the live plan (`borg_town_shadow.rs:51`). A
  shop lists true names; "unknown potion" is the name the message shows after
  the purchase (`{unseen}`). In 200 Ranger games all unknown-potion
  purchases were priced 20 gold (Cure Light Wounds) and all unknown scrolls
  15 gold (Phase Door). So the 43 percent "unknown potions" are healing, and the
  lever is `town_heal_*`; `town_unseen_percent` is the blunt coefficient the
  owner asked for.
- Phase Door in town is one curve whose first scroll is worth about a third of
  the escape weight (`borg_town_capability.rs:718`), outbid by 1-gold arrows
  and 20-gold potions; the reserve adds a requirement row (`:724`, `:537`).
- Phase Door on the floor: `take_select` skips a far item twice
  (`borg_flow_take.rs:562`, `:612`); the switch exempts a Phase Door.
