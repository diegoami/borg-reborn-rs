## 2026-11-23 – Preserve the step history at the 75-observation limit (#1106)

The step history now discards its oldest entry before appending to a full
history. The old code discarded a position as soon as the 75th observation
arrived and exposed an unwritten entry to readers for the next three
observations. The up-stair regression now feeds observations through the same
recorder as the Borg. It crosses the 75-entry limit on a search arrival and
checks the three positions used by the stair check.

This follow-up clarifies the scope of #1108. An up-stair search arrival can
be counted when a successful dark action targets the neighboring square just
visited. The count records the observed search arrival. A successful dark
action can queue a command that leaves the Borg on its current square.
The caution routines' bounce check can veto retreats based on health or
the anti-bounce counter. The dark and secret-door flows do not use that
retreat policy. The stair check uses the observed positions to qualify the
search count without vetoing the dark action.

In the fixture, a granite target causes the cost-one dark step to fail.
After five recorded search visits, neither the remaining dark step nor
the exhausted search queues another key at the adjacent floor square.
The live cause of the cost-one refusal and the next action after the
search budget is exhausted remain open in [#1106](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-reborn-rs/issues/1106).
The depth-9 diagnosis is tracked in [#1121](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-reborn-rs/issues/1121).
The game save starts earlier and reloads without the live Borg's decision
state. The uninterrupted recording is needed to trace the original cycle.
