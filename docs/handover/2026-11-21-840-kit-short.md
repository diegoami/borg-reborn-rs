## 2026-11-21: A "basic kit short" mode: the kit score, the kit leash and three restock remedies (#840)

Written on the cloud session's own branch `claude/wizardly-curie-u7y4zx`
(the session instruction), not `task/…` (RULES 5), from main `12c4ce7c`.

### What was done

`borg-core/src/borg_kit.rs` (ours, `OURS` in `scripts/borg-core-vendor.py`)
scores the kit and everything else hangs off the score. **Every switch is
compiled and shipped 0** (`harness/borg.txt`); the key streams of all 20 cases
at the shipped settings are byte-identical to main's.

- **The score:** the weighted mean of five shares, each what the Borg holds
  over its target, capped at 1: cures (CLW + CSW + CCW), Phase Door, a ranged
  option (launcher missiles over 20 or flasks of oil over 5, whichever is
  more; a Mage's or Necromancer's attack spell counts as met), turns of light
  (the wielded light and its spares: torches by their turns, flasks 7,500
  each for a lantern) and food. Targets, weights and the CL band split
  (CL1–5 / 6–15) are `kit_*` parameters; every number is a hypothesis taken
  from the issue's examples. Integers only (permille, an integer power), so a
  game replays from its keys.
- **`kit_leash`:** leash = `kit_leash_min` + (C's leash − `kit_leash_min`) ×
  score^`kit_leash_power`, while the score is below 1, in the dungeon, up to
  `kit_max_level` (15). It is `borg_get_leash`'s value, so every user of it
  follows: the explore, hunt and item flows, and `take_select`. The stair
  checks that refuse a hunt or detour beyond the leash are now on at any depth
  and level while the leash follows the kit. The "wandered too far, return to
  the up stair" block of `borg_think_dungeon` uses it too (never below 3).
  This is the "no detours outside the leash" half of the curiosity limits.
- **`kit_descent_min_percent`:** no descent below DL1 under that score
  (`borg_prepared`; from town the way to DL1 is never refused).
- **`kit_leave_danger_percent`:** short of kit, on an up staircase, at a danger
  of that share of the current HP the Borg takes the stairs (the kit twin of
  #614's rule), and off the stairs it starts fleeing (`borg_caution`).
- **Remedy a `kit_restock`:** the kit (score below `kit_restock_below_percent`,
  and with `kit_restock_light_turns` a light about to go out with no spare) is
  part of `borg_restock` at every depth, once the purse has grown by
  `kit_restock_min_gold` since the Borg left town. That condition is what
  keeps a Borg that left town short of kit (a store out of stock) from
  bouncing back at once.
- **Remedy b `kit_start_reserve`:** the live town planner keeps
  `kit_start_cure_reserve` cures (as hit points, as `town_heal_reserve_hp_percent`
  does) and `kit_start_phase_reserve` Phase Door as requirements. Per class
  with `borg_param_kit_start_reserve__<class>`.
- **Remedy c `kit_money_first`:** up to `kit_money_first_max_level` (2), short
  of kit and too poor: no descent below DL1 and a leash of at least
  `kit_money_leash` (6) for the gold near the stairs; a trip to town once the
  purse can pay. It needs `kit_leash` for the leash. The town hunt (#789,
  `town_hunt`) and money scum (`borg_money_scum_amount`) stay their own
  switches, which the zoo request lists as arms.
- **Notes:** with a switch on, a change of the score or the leash is noted
  once: `# Kit 0.60 (0/3 cures, 0/2 Phase, 57 missiles+0 oil, 14965 light
  turns, 5 food; short: cures, Phase): leash 8`.

### What was found

- **These replays say nothing about the kit.** One step of C's leash (the null
  arm, no kit code involved) turns 13 of the 17 deaths into survivals: a CL1
  game is chaotic, and the 17 were chosen because they died. The kit arms'
  8 (and `b`'s 17) are no better than that. They show that the code runs and
  what it does; whether it helps is for the zoo (RULES 14).
- **Mechanism, seen in the notes:** the notes show the score falling as cures
  and Phase Door are used and the leash following it (`# Kit 0.60 … leash 8`).
  `kit_leave_danger_percent` fired as "Fleeing (short of kit)" 34 times in the
  `kit` arm and never as "Leaving by the stairs" in these games.
- **Deaths the kit may not touch (corrected; checked in the base logs):** of the
  three Rangers killed by Fang or Grip, only Human Ranger 1718066060 (Grip)
  met the first contact with a kit at the issue's targets (3 CLW and 2
  Phase Door bought, contact at game turn 1014): a barely viable kit, not a
  complete one, since the targets are minimums. High-Elf Ranger 1024918585 (Fang) met it with
  2 CLW and 2 Phase Door (0.93: one cure short at turn 1113) and had spent
  both by 1261. Dwarf Ranger 1326879061 (Fang) is a kit-short case: its Phase
  Door ran out at turn 1943, Fang came at 2978 and it died at 3043. The
  earlier wording ("kit complete") was wrong for those two. The Mage and Druid
  killed by crows hold 0.75–0.92 kits (from the issue's table).
- **Remedy a is limited by money, as the issue says:** 13 of the 15 broke
  cases hold ≤10 gold, so no trip is affordable; it changes only the case that
  has gold.
- **Remedy b** bought 4 CLW and 1 Phase Door (not 2) for the Ranger 1024918585:
  the planner's budget decided between the reserve and the arrows. The reserve
  is valued as `town_phase_reserve` is; a higher value is the knob.
- **A tiny leash can leave a Borg with nothing to do** (it twitches and flees
  by the stairs, as C's bored Borg does). `kit_leash_min`, the power and
  `kit_money_leash` are there for the zoo to size; none is tuned.
- Cost: no measurable slowdown beyond the games being longer.
