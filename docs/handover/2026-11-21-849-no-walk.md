## #849: no desperate walk to the stairs from a faster fast-fragile monster

Follow-up of #847's review (finding 4) and of issue #842 item 4 ("no Desperate
for Stairs walk from a monster faster than the Borg"). Branch
`task/849-fast-fragile-no-walk`, pushed as `claude/youthful-brahmagupta-j7a2y1`.
Owner, 2026-11-21: "after this do 849".

### What was done
New switch `fast_fragile_no_walk`, compiled and **shipped 0**.
`desperate_for_stairs` (`borg_caution.rs`) first asks
`borg_fast_fragile::walk_blocked`: with the switch on and `attack_on_sight` on,
a fast-fragile monster (named, or by stats with `fast_fragile_by_stats`) that is
**faster than the Borg** and awake next to it refuses every walk of the function
(branches one, two, three, four and the known-down branch) and notes `# No walk
to the stairs from a faster fast-fragile attacker (#849); Fast-fragile (named):
crow (...)`. #747's `caution_desperate_fast_danger_percent` (shipped 50) covers
only branch three, above 50% of the HP; the other branches never looked at
speed. The attack, Phase Door and the other escapes decide instead.

### What was found
The 8 crow cases of 3b4924ba (`ca89e738`'s successors, this branch's build,
100,000 turns, switches appended to the case's `borg.txt`; direction only):

| arm | dead of 8 | to crow/cat |
|---|---:|---:|
| `off` (policy 0, ban 0: the play before #847) | 8 | 7 |
| `off` + `no_walk=1` | **6** | 5 |
| shipped (policy 1, ban 1) | 1 | 0 |
| shipped + `no_walk=1` | 1 | 0 (key streams identical in all 8) |

- Without the policy, the switch alone rescues **Necromancer 1705507033 and
  Half-Elf Druid 733550937**, the two games of #842 that walked ("Desperate for
  Stairs") from a crow twice their speed; 14 walks were refused.
- With the shipped policy on it changes nothing on these cases: the attack comes
  before `borg_caution`, so the walk is reachable only when the attack cannot
  act (no usable attack, other foes' danger over the gate). The switch is for
  those turns; no case here has one, so the zoo has to look for them.
- Smoke at the shipped settings (the line ships 0): 3/3 identical to
  `smoke-borg-rs`.

### What is left

  `borg_param_fast_fragile_no_walk=1`, and the same with `fast_fragile_by_stats=1`.
  Ship it on or off from that, not from these cases (RULES 14).
- The speed test is the engine's `mspeed` of the monster (with its observed
  Haste) against the Borg's `player_combat_speed`; a speed margin as in
  `fast_fragile_speed_margin` is not applied here (a monster 1 point faster
  that is marked still blocks the walk). A hypothesis; the walk is refused only
