## 2026-11-24 · Stair scumming and the mixed-walk radius cap (#1208)

All nine classes use stair scumming below 30 maximum HP while the
existing dungeon and detection conditions apply. Warriors inherit the
global switch and get four base slack steps. The other eight classes keep
their base slack. The per-level slack rate is unchanged.

The cutoff is exclusive. A character with exactly 30 max HP has already
left this mode. Mixed exploration stays on with a zero radius cap for
all classes. Mixed targets are limited to staircase squares, with the
existing exhausted-exploration exit when there is no such target.
Level growth keeps the cap at zero. The parameter accepts zero to
express this configuration.

The owner selected these settings after the
[exploration death investigation](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-cemetery/pull/155).
The changes have no population estimate for CL5 or deaths yet.
