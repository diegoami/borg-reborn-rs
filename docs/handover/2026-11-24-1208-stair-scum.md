## 2026-11-24 · Stair scumming and mixed exploration (#1208)

All nine classes use stair scumming below 30 maximum HP while the
existing dungeon and detection conditions apply. Warriors inherit the
global switch and get four base slack steps. The other eight classes
keep their base slack. The per-level slack rate is unchanged.

Mixed exploration starts at radius 2 and caps at 6 at CL1. The cap grows
by 1000 per mille per character level from CL1, giving caps of 6, 12,
18, 24, and 30 steps at CL1–5. The class ceilings rise by two levels. The
global sentinel stays at 50. Other eligibility checks and the existing
growth rates for start radius and step still apply.

The owner selected these settings after the
[exploration death investigation](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-cemetery/pull/155).
The changes have no population estimate for CL5 or deaths yet.
