## 2026-11-24 · Stair scumming below 30 max HP (#1208)

All nine classes use stair scumming below 30 maximum HP while the
existing dungeon and detection conditions apply. Warriors inherit the
global switch and have five base slack steps. The other eight classes
inherit zero base slack. The per-level slack rate and mixed exploration
values are retained.

The cutoff is exclusive. A character with exactly 30 max HP has already
left this mode. The owner selected these shipped settings after the
[exploration death investigation](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-cemetery/pull/155).
The change has no population estimate for CL5 or deaths yet.
