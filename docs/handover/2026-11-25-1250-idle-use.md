## 2026-11-25 — Use low-value consumables while idle (#1250)

The eight initial members of `IDLE_USE` have an inclusive minimum of
dungeon level 1. The owner changed the minimum from DL3 to DL1 and
added Potion of Slime Mold Juice. Each entry owns its depth. The existing
hunger action has priority, and idle use can spend juice before the next
hunger episode.

With `borg_param_item_use_idle = 1`, the Borg uses one item from the
smallest eligible known stack during dungeon inventory housekeeping.
Ties follow list order. A singleton can free a pack slot even when the
same kind has a future tactical use. Consecutive quiet turns can use
multiple items as the Borg reevaluates the pack on each think.
Combat, recovery, inferior-spare handling and ordinary junk disposal
have priority. Forced drops for pack space follow idle use.

Visible monsters, flight, Recall, an unseen attack, hallucination and
shapechange block idle use. A sleeping mimic still appears as an object
to the Borg. Unreadable scrolls leave potions eligible.

The owner directed shipping the switch at 1. The 0 control keeps
ordinary Magic Mapping on its original schedule. At 1, the inventory
mapping read follows identification, enchantment and recharging and
requires a quiet turn. The existing wall-detection action can still
read mapping scrolls on its own schedule.

The old draft's CI exposed shifted parameter citations and changed
the exact turn of a historical stair handoff. The new helper follows
the existing item-use code to keep its citations stable. The historical
test pins idle use off and preserves the original route.
The historical unknown-consumable sale replays also pin 0. With the
shipped profile at 1, the Warrior smoke run quaffs Berserk Strength
on DL1 at turn 681. The smoke reference was re-recorded.
