## 2026-11-25 — Use low-value consumables while idle (#1250)

The shipped Borg tries `IDLE_USE` during dungeon inventory housekeeping,
after combat and recovery and before ordinary pack disposal. An eligible
known stack can be used at or above its own minimum dungeon depth. All
initial entries use dungeon level 3. The Borg uses one item from the
smallest eligible stack and reevaluates on its next think.
It skips the housekeeping action when a monster is on the current panel,
during flight or Recall, while dealing with an unseen attacker, and when
hallucinating or shapechanged. Unreadable scrolls leave potions eligible.

Scroll of Blessing joins the six requested kinds because it gives a
shorter version of Holy Chant's buff (`engine/angband/lib/gamedata/object.txt`).
Magic Mapping moves from unconditional inventory use to `IDLE_USE`. The
separate detection action can still read a copy when walls need mapping.

The unit regression exercises the inclusive per-item depth, differing
depths for two items, mixed stack sizes, unreadable scrolls and unknown
items. Native Human Warrior starts with prepared packs confirmed that the
Borg reads or quaffs a singleton first at dungeon level 3 and frees its
slot. Idle use stayed off at dungeon level 2 and started after combat in
the adjacent white worm mass scenario. The shipped smoke suite matched
all four reference runs.
