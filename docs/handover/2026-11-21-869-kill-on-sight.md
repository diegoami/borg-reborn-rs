Owner, 2026-11-21, after playing borg-zoo#581's Gnome Necromancer 459443830

- **`kill-on-sight`**, a reason of an attack entry (`KILL_ON_SIGHT`,
  `borg_fight_attack.rs`): attacked on sight in `fight_before_flight`, before
  the cures and Phase Door, without the target's own damage test; the other
  monsters' danger still stops it (`category_attack_safe_with(.., false)`).
- **Attack kinds**, an optional sixth field of an attack entry
  (`scary_list::ATTACK_KINDS`: melee, missiles, throws, spells, devices,
  activations; none = all). `focused_attack` ranks only those (best first),
  so attack on sight, fast-fragile and the winning-fight test (#759) all
  respect it for that monster.
- **Shipped:** `grey mushroom patch | 50 | | attack | kill-on-sight | melee,
  missiles, throws, devices`. Throws were added to the owner's "melee,
  missiles, wands" for the flask of oil; spells left out.
