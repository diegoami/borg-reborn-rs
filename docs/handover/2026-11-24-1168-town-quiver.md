## Plain quiver surplus and town headroom (#1168)

The opt-in trial removes whole stacks of plain ammunition for the equipped
launcher while above the carry allowance. It runs after the urgent checks
and before a plan would leave or dispose of other carried kit. In this
trial, dropping ammunition destroys it. We put that step before Home
storage or sale to test whether reducing plain surplus preserves trip
supplies. The order and a nonzero reserve are still candidate policy.

The trim skips gear with unknown runes, bonuses, curses, ego or artifact
status, or any brace annotation in its description. It rereads the quiver
after each drop. Choose and execution both require the reserve to be
enabled. The compiled and shipped reserve remains 0.

The fixture uses 40 plain shots because the native maximum stack in one
quiver slot is 40 (constants.txt, carry-cap:quiver-slot-size). At
0.4 lb each, that is 16 lb. The 5 lb departure-room goal is separate.
The nominal 300-pebble fixture begins with 210 plain and 90 enchanted
shots. The expected trim stops at 40 plain and 90 enchanted.
