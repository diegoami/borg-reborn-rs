## Plain quiver surplus and town headroom (#1168)

The candidate lets the live town planner unload whole stacks of known,
uninscribed, unenchanted ammunition for the equipped launcher when
already over the weight allowance. It runs when the plan would
dispose of pack stock or leave. It preserves the configured plain
reserve and leaves unidentified, inscribed, cursed and special ammo
alone. The town planner rereads the kit after each drop. Whole-stack
disposal leaves the retained ammunition's inscription intact.

`town_quiver_trim_reserve` is compiled and shipped at **0**. Use a
candidate of **40** for the named case. The pre-release smoke suite
on the default borg-rs core matched all four shipped references.
Both Rust test suites, Clippy, formatting and parameter citations pass.

The [Cemetery fixtures and receipts](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-cemetery/tree/bdc19c09140d5a0a210bde95abe9796ba5a3ae8f/scenarios/late-inventory-mage-10815143)
include a CL37 Mage with 300 pebbles, a matched 40-pebble start, and
later CL45 continuations. The candidate is a partial repair. Verify
the reserve across classes and distinguish remaining quiver weight
from worn gear and protected kit before activation. The class-level
inventory audit is #810. [Zoo #764](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-zoo/issues/764)
tracks device retention; a separate candidate arm would measure this
change.
