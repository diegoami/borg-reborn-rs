## 2026-11-24: Ship breadth-first exploration off for P8 (#1201)

The owner approved setting `explore_breadth_first=0` with mixed exploration
enabled. Mixed can apply whenever its own class cap and leash allow it.
Breadth-first's conditional food and light boost is inactive at these settings.

The [Zoo small tier](https://diegoami.github.io/borg-zoo/runs/reborn-p8-exploration-small/45393358980-1/index.html)
on `c5501d3c` had 83/90 reach CL5 by 100,000 turns with breadth-first off
versus 63/90 with both modes enabled. The 90 paired seeds gave 24 gains
and four losses. Neither arm had a death, leaving safety unresolved.
The larger paired tiers remain tracked by
[Zoo #754](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-zoo/issues/754).
The readiness design (#1080) and breadth-first loop (#1189) remain separate.
