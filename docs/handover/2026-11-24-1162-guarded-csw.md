# #1162 guarded Cure Serious Wounds preference

The live borg-rs town planner can optionally give CSW a purchase bonus once
the pack covers the fuel goal, the nutrition floor and the Phase Door reserve.
Urgent Recall and any affordable missing Recall reserve take precedence. The
normal solver still checks stock, gold, pack room and weight. An existing
full confusion cure supplies the first copy. The next copy has one quarter
of the bonus, and later copies only get their ordinary value.

The new parameter, `town_csw_reserve_percent`, ships at 0. Use 100 as a
hypothesis against shipped and the unguarded one-copy confusion reserve.
The original #752 pilot sped up some purchases but left measured departure
coverage unchanged. #1162 needs the first affordable opportunity, actual
CSW on departure, and Phase Door and Recall from the same visit. Preserve
the cheap CLW option on short gold or when CSW is out of stock.

Run the named cases with the shipped borg-rs core and
`BORG_TOWN_PLAN_RECORD` to inspect the competing baskets. Cross-start
measurement belongs to borg-zoo #752. Enabling a shipped value is a separate
owner decision once that evidence is read.
