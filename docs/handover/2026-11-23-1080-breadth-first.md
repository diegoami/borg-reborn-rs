## 2026-11-23: breadth-first exploration from the staircases (#1085, from #1080)

**Done.** Four settings, all compiled 0, in
`borg_flow_dark.rs`: `explore_breadth_first` (0), `explore_bfs_max_hp` (25),
`explore_bfs_hp_per_level` (0) and `explore_bfs_slack` (0). The names are
#1085's. The owner chose the threshold's shape (2026-11-23): max HP below
`explore_bfs_max_hp` + `explore_bfs_hp_per_level` x CL, both per class.
**Shipped on** (owner, 2026-11-23: "ship it like that and you can merge it
and turn it on by default"): `explore_breadth_first = 1` in
`harness/borg.txt` and the per-class list of `docs/CALIBRATION.md` in the
class files. `smoke-borg-rs` is re-recorded. The list is not measured
(RULES 14); the zoo compares it with `explore_breadth_first = 0`.

When the switch is on and the Borg's max HP is below the threshold, both
exploration calls pick their target by its *ring*: its flow steps from the
nearest known staircase, up or down, over known grids.
- The ring nearest the stairs goes first, up to its ring + slack. Ties go to
  the grid nearest the Borg.
- A path never crosses a ring farther out than its goals' or the Borg's own,
  nor a grid past the "wandered too far" return leash, now shared as
  `borg_think_dungeon::return_leash`.
- A staircase newly seen drops a committed exploration flow, so the next
  think plans with the new rings (`borg_update`).
- When the Borg itself has no route to a staircase, C's methods run.

The rules are in `docs/CALIBRATION.md` ("Breadth-first exploration from the
staircases").
