## 2026-11-23: Black Market access and town reserves (#1103)

Owner: "keep and buy whenever it finds on the black market up from a certain
budget" (the potions, staves, rods and wands the core uses, and armour, rings
and amulets with a resistance or ability not worn yet); "make it a parameter
of course, but I would put them much lower, flat 6000 starting with a level
negative delta of 100".

PR #1112 includes Track P at `3c46575d166426074d128653e6c4f861997e6f02`.
P8 ships the candidate changes off. The nominal life-reserve depth stays 12.

- The ordinary Black Market floor uses `max(0, min_gold - level_delta * CL)`.
  The plan record and ordinary solver use the thresholded share. The urgent
  needs and checkout use the configured cap. The plan is rebuilt on entry.
- The Restore Life Levels reserve starts at the configured depth without a
  repeatable spell or activation. The floor gates preventive shop value.
  Owned potions and free Home withdrawals count at every purse. Actual XP
  drain retains its existing treatment value.
- The town reserve for one Vigor or Purging applies while no stat is drained,
  for a stat the mushroom could restore. Each offered mushroom counts as
  one copy even at a discounted unseen-flavour setting. DL6 is a candidate
  choice. The radiation eye already drains Strength at DL3.
- The new class flag governs paid offers of stat potions that lower another
  stat. The existing consumption policy is used to determine eligibility.

The [MiniMax re-review at `ddc385bc`](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-reborn-rs/pull/1112#issuecomment-7130012883)
found the old gold check read the healthy-or-restorable trait and therefore
allowed a healthy Borg to buy for 400 under a 1,000-gold floor. The patch
separates eligibility and paid supply. Rust tests also cover the Black Market
cap, the ordinary and urgent paths on one stock, the mushroom unit count,
and preservation of the existing town capability row IDs.

[Native checkout cases and replay](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-cemetery/tree/main/scenarios/town-restore-life-1103)
use one constructed Mage start as a controlled experiment. The replays
change the floor or purse and record the engine's purchase confirmation.
