## Evaluation and diagnosis

See the [overall assessment](overall-assessment.md). A CL2 Half-Troll Necromancer spent 300 of
its 360 starting gold on one body armour, bought one Phase Door and no cure, and left its Recall
at Home. On DL2 a single apprentice set whole-level flight. Walking down the stair corridor with
the attackers behind it, seven squares from the stairs, it read its only Phase Door and landed on
the far side of the group. Cornered at a junction, it meleed a stream of cutpurses and apprentices
for about 190 turns. A Phase Door dropped on an adjacent square was never picked up, because
attack runs before item pickup. The owner's verified takeover from 7703 survived by a lucky Phase
onto the stair corridor. These are observed and source-checked mechanisms; no reusable
original-C defect or rescue is established.
